to said Negroes death or otherwise, while they are in the possession of the said Mary Southerland

Know all men by these presents that
I George Washington McKeith of
Richmond District and state aforesaid (formerly of the State of Georgia)
and Mary Southerland and others of the State of South Carolina and District of Richmond held
Certain Money in trust administration to pay off the penal sum of five thousand
Dollars to which payment well and truly to be made. I bind myself my heirs Executors and
Administrators firmly by these presents sealed with my seal and dated this twenty
fifth day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty three
The Condition of the above obligation is such that the above bound Negro Washington
McKeith his heirs Executors Administrators or assigns shall and do well and truly
on or by the twenty fifth day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and thirty five give and affix unto and title to his interest to the said
plantation property slaves, stock of every kind and description whatsoever, the said
house being the plantation whereon Ann Southerland decd formerly lived and
whereon now is this day, below after the said Ann of his part of said Estate and
to hold and enjoy and forever to the heirs of Mary Southerland and others
In Witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal the day and year above
written to be held and to be made in full force and virtue
signed sealed and delivered in the presence of us by Robert Holsey before signed
Test Robert Holsey George W. McKeith (seal)
W. L. Keith C.C.

State of Carolina Personally appeared Robert Holsey before me made oath that he saw
Richmond District and the said George McKeith sign seal and deliver the within
Mortgage for the use and purpose within mentioned and saw Ann Southerland as a
subscribing witness to the same sworn to the 25th day of Jany. 1834
