And the above mentioned George McKeith further agrees that if any misfortune shall happen
to said Negroes death or otherwise, while they are in the possession of the said Mary Southerland
that it shall be his loss and nothing in this above obligation any misfortunes that may happen to
the said Negroes that they remain as they are. December 1833
Signed Sealed and acknowledged in the presence of
W. L. Keith
Test Robert Holsey, John Southerland

State of Carolina Personally appeared Robert Holsey an made oath that he saw
Robert Holsey George McKeith sign seal and deliver the within Mortgage for the
use and purpose within mentioned and that he saw John Southerland do the same
a subscribing witness to the same sworn to the 3rd day of Jany 1834
W. L. Keith C.C.
Robert Holsey
Recorded the 3rd day of February 1834 and Examined by
William L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.

George McKeith
to
Mary Southerland & others

The State of South Carolina
Pickens District

Know all men by these presents that
I George Washington McKeith of
Pickens District and state aforesaid (formerly of the State of Georgia)
and Mary Southerland and others of the State of South Carolina and District of Pickens heirs
Certain Attorney Executors Administrators assigns in the penal sum of five thousand
Dollars to which payment well and truly to be made. I bind myself my heirs Executors and
Administrators firmly by these presents sealed with my seal and dated this twentieth
day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty three
The Condition of the above obligation is such that the above bound Negro Washington
McKeith his heirs Executors Administrators or assigns shall and do well and truly
on or by the twenty fifth day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and thirty five give and affix unto and title to his interest to the said
plantation piece of land, (same year) and one negro woman named Mary Southerland
same being the plantation wherein Ann Southerland decd formerly lived and
wherein now is this day, both of the said Ann of his part of said estate to the said
to hold and enjoy forever to the heirs of Mary Southerland
