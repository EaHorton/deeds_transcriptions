And the above mentioned George McKeith further agrees that if any misfortune shall happen
to said Negroes death or otherwise, while they are in the possession of the said Mary Louthan
that it shall be his loss and nothing in this act shall prevent any misfortune that may happen to
the said Negroes that they remain as they [?] December 1833
Signed Sealed and acknowledged in the presence of
W. L. Keith
Test Robert Holsey, John Louthan

State of Carolina Personally appeared Robert Holsey an made oath that he saw
Robert Holsey George McKeith sign seal and deliver the within Mortgage for the
uses purposes within mentioned and that he with John Louthan in the presence
a subscribing witness to the same sworn to the 3rd day of Jany 1834
W. L. Keith C.C.
Robert Holsey
Recorded the 3rd day of February 1834 and Examined by
William L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.

Know all men by these presents that
I George Washington McKeith of
Pickens District and state aforesaid (formerly of the State of Georgia)
and Mary Louthan and others of the State of South Carolina and Pickens District
Certain Attorney being bound administrators & assigns in the penal sum of five thousand
Dollars to which payment well and truly to be made. I bind myself my heirs Executors and
Administrators firmly by these presents sealed with my seal and dated this twentieth
day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty three
The Condition of the above obligation is such that the above bound Negro Washington
McKeith his heirs Executors Administrators & assigns shall and do well and truly
on or by the twenty fifth day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and thirty five give and sufficient unto and title to his interest to the said
plantation piece of land, (same of one hundred and fifty acres) and the said
Louthan being the plantation whereon Ann Louthan decd formerly lived and
whereon now is this day, both of the said Ann of his part of said Estate and
the Louthan agreement between the heirs of said Ann Louthan decd
Go Louthan shall make a cause to be made the said title above mentioned then this
obligation to be void or else to remain in full force and virtue
signed sealed and delivered in the presence of us by Robert Louthan before signed
Test Robert Holsey
Robert Holsey
State of Carolina Personally appeared Robert Holsey before me made oath that he saw
Robert Holsey and the said George McKeith sign seal and deliver the within mentioned
bond for the use and purposes within mentioned and saw Ann Louthan and a
subscribing witness to the same sworn to the 3rd day of Jany. 1834
Before Wm. L. Keith C.C.
Robert Holsey
Recorded the 3rd day of February 1834 and Examined by
William L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.
