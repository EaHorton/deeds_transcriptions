To Thomas S. Keese & Washington
T. Keith of the District and State
aforesaid formerly of the State of Georgia Lumpkin County
Know ye that I George W. Keith of said State and County
for and in consideration of the sum of Two Thousand dollars to Mary Sutherland & others
I George W. Keith bind myself that I will make or cause to be made a good and sufficient right and
title to the interest in the plantation or piece of land known and distinguished as the
formerly known and now free adjoining known there is a good title to Mary Sutherland & Co
known Childs now being the Estate of Thomas Sutherland Dec’d. And for the security
of the performance of the above I do hereby assign and transfer all my right
of claim in and upon whatsoever claim I have or may have to the said Mary Sutherland & Co
their heirs or assigns the following property to wit: One negro girl named Amy
about ten years of age, the following property to wit: One negro girl named Amy
about ten years of age, One negro woman named Amy, One negro woman named Amy
is attached to me being my wife and one of Jack Estate I have and hold and own
during her natural life and I do hereby bind myself my heirs Executors administrators
to warrant and forever defend the said farm Negroes to the said Mary Sutherland &
Co their heirs and assigns from myself and assigns forever. I do further bind myself
my heirs and assigns to make all lawful endeavors lawfully claiming the same or
in any part thereof. Provided always nevertheless and it is hereby agreed by and
between the said parties that if the said George W. Keith shall make or cause to be made
unto by the twenty fifth day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and thirty five a good and sufficient right and title to a certain plantation or
a tract of land known Thomas Sutherland now formerly known and a small field
adjoining known there is a Good title to the said Thomas Sutherland & minor Child
the said George W. Keith his heirs Executors and administrators to him in a case of
agreement in the assignment of the Estate of the said Thomas Sutherland Dec’d and
the said George W. Keith further agrees to deliver the said four Negroes to the said
Mary Sutherland & Co for them to remain in the hands of possession until the title
is made on the 25th Dec 1835 or until the said title of perfected according to
law and should the said G. W. Keith attempt to take the said property out of possession
of the said Mary Sutherland & Co before the said time, it is agreed that the
State of South Carolina in the said right and interest it may be proper and correct to
the said Mary Sutherland & Co and it is understood and agreed that the said
G. W. Keith the property remains in possession of the said Mary Sutherland &
heirs or assigns until the said title is made according to law then this agreement
to be void and of no effect. Otherwise shall extend to him and his heirs
until such void & all interest and property Otherwise to continue in full force and
virtue in law in the keeping whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this
twenty second day of October in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred
and thirty five and in the fifty eighth year of American Independence
Signed sealed and delivered in the presence of
J. W. Keith
