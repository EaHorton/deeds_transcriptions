the ninth day of June one thousand eight hundred and fifty and 
in the seventy fifth year of the sovereignty and 
independence of the United States of America 
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of 
Thos Williams Elisha Thomas 
South Carolina } Before me personally appeared 
Pickens District } Thomas Williams and made oath that he saw the within named 
Elijah Coates sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within 
instrument of writing for the uses and purposes therein mentioned 
and that he with Elisha Thomas in the presence of each other 
witnessed the due execution thereof sworn to and subscribed before me this 5 day of July 1851 
Thos H. McCann N.P. 
Recorded the 15 day of June 1852 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.

Know all men by these 
presents that I William P. Steele of Pickens 
District and State aforesaid for and in consideration of the sum 
of thirty dollars to me in hand paid by Rev Elijah Coates 
of William District and State aforesaid have granted bargained 
sold and released and by these presents do grant bargain sell 
and release unto the said Elijah Coates his heirs and assigns 
forever all that piece parcel or tract of land situate in Pickens 
District and State aforesaid on waters of George's Creek waters of 
Keowee River the same being part of a tract of land granted to 
Pickens as the property of Thomas Steele containing fifty 
acres more or less reference to the Creek for a more perfect 
view respectfully } Together with all and singular the rights 
members hereditaments and appurtenances to the said premises 
belonging or in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to 
hold all singular the said premises before mentioned unto the said Elijah Coates 
his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind myself my heirs 
executors and administrators to warrant and forever defend all and 
singular the said premises unto the said Elijah Coates his heirs and 
assigns against myself and my heirs and against every person whomsoever 
lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. Witness my hand 
and seal this 5 day of July 1851 

Signed sealed and delivered in presence of 
Thos H. Keith W. L. Keith
