This Indenture made the first day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty five between John Henderson of the State and District aforesaid of the one part and R. A. Henderson of the same place of the other part Witnesseth that the said John Henderson for and in consideration of the sum of one hundred and sixty five dollars to him in hand paid by the said R. A. Henderson at and before the sealing and delivery of these presents the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged hath granted bargained sold and released and by these presents doth grant bargain sell and release unto the said R. A. Henderson all that plantation or tract of land containing one hundred and fifty acres more or less situate lying and being in the District of Edgefield on the waters of Stevens Creek and Bounded by lands of James G. Norris, James H. Glover, and others. Together with all and singular the rights members hereditaments and appurtenances to the said premises belonging or in any wise incident or appertaining To have and to hold all and singular the premises before mentioned unto the said R. A. Henderson his heirs and assigns forever And the said John Henderson for himself his heirs executors and administrators doth covenant with the said R. A. Henderson his heirs and assigns that he the said John Henderson is lawfully seized in fee simple of the premises aforesaid that they are free from all incumbrances and that he hath good right to sell and convey the same to the said R. A. Henderson his heirs and assigns forever And further that he the said John Henderson his heirs executors and administrators will warrant and forever defend the said premises unto the said R. A. Henderson his heirs and assigns against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever. Provided always and these presents are upon this condition that if the said John Henderson his heirs executors or administrators shall and do well and truly pay or cause to be paid unto the said R. A. Henderson his heirs executors administrators or assigns the full and just sum of one hundred and sixty five dollars on or before the first day of January which will be in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty six with lawful interest for the same then and from thenceforth as well this present Indenture and the right to the property thereby conveyed as also the said recited obligation shall cease determine and be utterly void to all intents and purposes anything herein contained to the contrary notwithstanding. In witness whereof the said John Henderson hath hereunto set his hand and seal the day and year first above written.

Personally appeared before me John C. Allen and made oath that he saw the within named John Henderson sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within written deed of conveyance for the uses and purposes therein mentioned and that he with R. A. Henderson in the presence of each other witnessed the due execution thereof.
