Mortgage of Personal Property
South Carolina Know all men by these presents that in
Pickens District Consideration that J. O. Lewis is jointly
responsible to the note for the sum of two thousand dollars
now in order to secure him against the payment of the aforesaid sum
of two thousand dollars I do hereby bargain sell and deliver
unto him the said J. O. Lewis the following property to wit: one
negro boy by name of Caroline. To have and to hold the said boy Caroline
and the said J. O. Lewis his heirs and assigns forever. Provided
that if the said sum of two thousand dollars at that a certain note and
bearing date with this is paid at maturity then this obligation
to be null and void otherwise to remain in full force and virtue.
Test W. L. Keith F. H. Garvin (seal)
Jas H. Burr
Feby 3, 1849
South Carolina Personally appeared before me W. L. Keith and made oath
Pickens District that he saw F. H. Garvin sign seal and as his act and
deed deliver the within deed to J. O. Lewis for the use and purposes therein
mentioned sworn to before me 5 day of Octo 1852
Wm. Halland N. P.
Recorded the 5 day of Octo 1852 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C. & M. E.

Release For 280 Acres Land
The State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents
Pickens District that I Joseph Burnell of the State
and District aforesaid for and in Consideration of the sum of
five hundred dollars to me paid by D. Van Gilson in the State aforesaid
which have granted bargained sold and released and by these presents do grant
bargain sell and release unto the said D. Van Gilson his heirs and assigns
or tract of land situate in the State and District aforesaid being on the
waters of Twelve Mile and containing two hundred and eighty acres more or less
and bounded on the North by lands of John Smith on the East by lands of
David Anderson on the South by lands of James Brown and on the West by lands
of William Jones. Together with all and singular the rights members hereditaments
and appurtenances to the said premises belonging or in anywise incident or
appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the premises before mentioned
unto the said D. Van Gilson his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind
myself my heirs executors and administrators to warrant and forever defend all
and singular the said premises unto the said D. Van Gilson his heirs and assigns
against myself and my heirs and against every person whomsoever lawfully claiming
or to claim the same or any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this 3rd day of
March in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifty two and in the
76th year of the Independence of the United States of America.
Sealed and delivered in the presence of
W. L. Keith Joseph Burnell (seal)
Jas H. Burr
Recorded the 5th day of Octo 1852 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C. & M. E.
