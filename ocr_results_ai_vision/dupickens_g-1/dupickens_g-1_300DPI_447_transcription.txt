Trustee. William D. Keith for the use of my said wife & children as aforesaid all the goods money & debts
and chattels real and personal estate that may be in the hands of said Wm. D. Keith administrator of the
Estate of J. Halsey decd that may be made or coming from said Estate and the said property shall be in
the hands power or possession of any other person. The said Wm. D. Keith as trustee is to continue the
same and to hold as trust estate for my said wife and children and to manage and use said trust estate
for the use of my said wife & children or in trust the same in any other kind of property that
will more advantageously in his judgement secure the benefit of said wife & children. And if at any
meeting of the said Wm. D. Keith trustee shall see fit to have the said trust estate or any part thereof sold
and to reinvest the same in any other kind of property he may think fit and proper for my
said wife & children and in case of the death of my said wife or any of my said children the said Wm. D. Keith is
hereby authorized and empowered to appoint other trustee or trustees to act for him to carry
out this trust. Now this said trustee is to be fully discharged from all liability on account of
the said deed as witness my hand and seal this sixth day of March in the year of our Lord
one thousand eight hundred and fifty four and in the seventy eighth year of American independence.
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of
Wm. Halsey (Seal)
South Carolina } Wm. D. Keith the Trustee named in the annexed or within deed of trust
Pickens District } as hereof signing my acceptance of the trust therein specified and stated.
Given under my hand and seal this 1st day of March A.D. 1854.
W. D. Keith (Seal)
