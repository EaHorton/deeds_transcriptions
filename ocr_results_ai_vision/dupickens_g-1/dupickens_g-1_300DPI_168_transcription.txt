Bond
The State of South Carolina}
Pickens District } Know all men by these
presents that I Joseph Smith
am held and firmly bound unto Basil Smith of the District
of Pickens District in the full and just sum of one thousand dollars to be
paid to the said Basil Smith his Certain Attorney, or assigns to which pay-
ment well and truly to be made and done I bind myself my heirs executors
and administrators firmly by these presents sealed with my seal and dated
this twenty third day of May in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred
and fifty five and in the seventy ninth year of the Independence of the United States of America.
The Condition of the above Obligation is such that if the above bound Joseph Smith
his heirs executors or administrators shall and do well and truly make
or cause to be made unto the said Basil Smith his heirs executors or assigns
a good and sufficient deed of conveyance for that degree or parcel of land
we within and we have this day bargained and sold to the said Basil Smith
his heirs and assigns then this obligation to be void or else to remain in full
force and virtue. Joseph Smith
Witness Sealed and delivered in presence of

Release for 1100 Acres Land
State of South Carolina
This Indenture made the fourth day of
January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred
and sixty three between Nathan Boon of the County and State aforesaid
Court of Equity for Pickens District at Pickens Court House in the said State to
and with Robert Anderson of Pickens District in the said State of South
Carolina farmer of the other part. Witnesseth that the said Nathan
Boon for and in consideration of the sum of one thousand dollars to him in
hand paid by the said Robert Anderson at and before the sealing and delivery
of these presents the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged hath granted
bargained sold released and confirmed and by these presents doth grant
bargain sell release and confirm unto the said Robert Anderson in his
actual possession now being and to his heirs and assigns forever all that
plantation or tract of land situate lying and being in the District of Pickens
and State aforesaid on the waters of Twelve Mile Creek containing eleven
hundred acres more or less and bounded by lands of the estate of Benjamin
B. Holcombe deceased and others. Together with all and singular the
rights members hereditaments and appurtenances to the said premises
belonging or in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all
and singular the premises before mentioned unto the said Robert Anderson
his heirs and assigns forever. And the said Nathan Boon for himself his
heirs executors and administrators doth hereby covenant and agree to and
with the said Robert Anderson his heirs and assigns that he the said Nathan
Boon his heirs executors and administrators the said plantation or tract of
land and premises hereby granted bargained sold and released unto the said
Robert Anderson his heirs and assigns against himself and his heirs and
against every person or persons whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim
the same or any part thereof shall and will warrant and forever defend by
these presents. In witness whereof the said Nathan Boon hath hereunto set
his hand and seal the day and year first above written.
