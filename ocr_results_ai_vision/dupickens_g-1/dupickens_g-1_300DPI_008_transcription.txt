Deed of Gift of Negro Girl
State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents that
Pickens District I William Mayfield of the
District and State aforesaid of the one part, and Benjamin Mauldin and
Hannah Mauldin his present Wife of the other part, Witnesseth that the said
William Mayfield in consideration of the love and affection which he bears
unto the said Hannah Mauldin for and, in consideration of one negro girl
named Charlotte being a legacy or a bequest to the said Hannah Mauldin
by her late husband, and for other good causes and considerations him there-
unto moving, hath given and granted, and by these presents doth give and
grant unto the said Benjamin Mauldin and Hannah Mauldin his wife
one negro girl named Charlotte, to have and to hold the said negro girl
Charlotte unto the said Benjamin Mauldin and Hannah Mauldin his
wife, their heirs and assigns forever. And the said William Mayfield doth
hereby bind himself his heirs executors and administrators to warrant and
forever defend the said negro girl Charlotte unto the said Benjamin Mauldin
and Hannah Mauldin his wife, their heirs and assigns against himself and
his heirs and against every other person or persons whomsoever lawfully
claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In witness whereof
the said William Mayfield hath hereunto set his hand and seal this 1st day
of October Eighteen hundred and fifty one.
William Mayfield (seal)
Signed Sealed and delivered in presence of
James L. Perry Joshua Jameson Hewit Ellis
South Carolina Personally appeared before me William Mayfield and
Pickens District made oath that he was present and saw William Mayfield sign
seal and deliver the within deed of Gift and that he was a subscribing witness thereunto
Sworn and subscribed this 1st day of October 1851
before me W. L. Keith C.C.
Recorded the 17th day of Octb. 1851 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C

Mortgage of Real Estate
State of South Carolina To all whom these presents may come
Pickens District Know that I Andrew H. Sloan of the
District of Greenville S.C. am held and firmly bound unto
Andrew H. Sloan in the sum of one thousand dollars to be paid to the said
E. F. Robins his heirs executors administrators or assigns, to which payment
well and truly to be made I bind myself my heirs executors and administra-
tors firmly by these presents. Sealed with my seal and dated this 1st day
of August 1851. The condition of the above obligation is such that whereas
the said Andrew H. Sloan hath this day executed and delivered unto the said
E. F. Robins his certain bond or obligation bearing even date herewith
whereby the said Andrew H. Sloan is held and firmly bound unto the said
E. F. Robins in the sum of one thousand dollars conditioned for the payment
of the sum of five hundred dollars on or before the 1st day of August 1852
with interest from the date thereof at the rate of seven per cent per annum
being the purchase money for the following property to wit: all that tract or
parcel of land situate lying and being in the District of Pickens and State
aforesaid on the waters of Saluda River containing one hundred and fifty
acres more or less bounded by lands of John P. Sloan, E. F. Robins and others
and being the same tract of land conveyed to the said Andrew H. Sloan by
John P. Sloan by deed bearing date the 1st day of August 1851 and now
of record in the office of the Register of Mesne Conveyance for Pickens District
in Book No. 2 page 123. Now the condition of the above obligation is such
that if the said Andrew H. Sloan his heirs executors or administrators shall
well and truly pay or cause to be paid unto the said E. F. Robins his heirs
executors administrators or assigns the said sum of five hundred dollars
on or before the 1st day of August 1852 with interest thereon at the rate of
seven per cent per annum according to the condition of the said bond or
obligation then this obligation to be void or else to remain in full force and
effect. In witness whereof the said Andrew H. Sloan hath hereunto set his
hand and seal the day and year first above written.
Andrew H. Sloan (seal)
