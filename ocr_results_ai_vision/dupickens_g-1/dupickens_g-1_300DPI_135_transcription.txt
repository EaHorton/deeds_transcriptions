State of South Carolina Personally appeared David Cherry before me and
Pickens District 3 made oath that he did see James Simmons sign seal
and as act and deed deliver the within deed for the use and purpose therein
mentioned and that Thos. H. Tyer with himself did subscribe his name
to the same Sworn and subscribed before me 18th day of Aug 1834
J. H. R. Covardice clk. D. David Cherry
Plat of the above Release

South Carolina At the request of John Bates I have platse the piece of land
Pickens District that I surveyed in the plat that it bounds courses and c. certify
certify that it is a correct representation of a piece of land that was
originally granted to Daniel mason and that I have surveyed from several
corners of Daniel mason's land and from David mason's land from David
simmons's land from James simmons's land from James simmons to John Bates's who
now lives on the said land and from James simmons to John Bates's who
being when surveyed in the year of our Lord 1834 Given under my hand this 10th
day of March 1834
