To all whom these presents shall come I
Andrew P. Calhoun of Pickens District and State aforesaid send
Greeting whereas the said Andrew P. Calhoun by my certain
Bond or obligation bearing date the 18th day of July one thousand eight hundred and fifty
four I and firmly held and bound unto Floride L. Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun
of the same State and District in the penal sum of ninety eight thousand dollars condi-
tioned for the payment of the full just and legal sum of forty five thousand dollars that is to
say forty thousand two hundred dollars to the said Floride L. Calhoun and eight thousand
eight hundred dollars to the said Cornelia L. Calhoun the whole amount to be paid
in fifteen years from the said 18th day of July 1854 in equal annual instalments of
Fifty five hundred dollars commencing with interest on the whole amount of the
sum in equal annual instalments thereafter with interest on the whole amount of the
first ten years at the rate of five and one half per cent per annum and on the remain-
ing years at the rate of five per cent per annum until the said instalments of the full
due payment to be punctually made at the Bank of Charleston and failing at
such time and place aforesaid or such amount as shall be fully offset by the
said Bond in condition thereof reference being thereunto had Now Know all men
That I the said Andrew P. Calhoun in consideration of the said debt and sum of money specified
and for the better securing the payment thereof to the said Floride Calhoun and Cornelia L.
Calhoun according to the condition of the said Bond and also in consideration of the
sum of five dollars to me paid by the said Floride L. Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun
the said Andrew P. Calhoun have bargained and sold and by these presents do bargain
and sell unto the said women named certain lands in the State of Florida to wit
Andrew P. Calhoun in following particularly 31 negroes, 7 other such articles found in
described as follows Moses Jasper Duncan Armstead Jacob Tom Sato by Bonnie
Louisa Clary Silas Dicey Fanny Hannah Dinah Kizzy Joe George July Sarah Judy
Peggy probably Mary Delphin Sally Edward Amy Isaac Jacob Amity Hetty and
child Mary Richmond Abby Lucy Andrew and Jackson fifty in number and more
particularly described in a schedule annexed to have and to hold the said tracts of land to
said Floride Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun their executors administrators and assigns
forever Provided always nevertheless that if the said Andrew P. Calhoun his heirs executors
administrators shall and do well and truly pay or cause to be paid unto the said
Floride Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun their executors administrators or assigns the
said sum of fifty five hundred dollars in money of good and lawful current money of
currency according to the true intent and meaning of the said bond and condition
therein written then this deed of bargain and sale and all and every article
clause and thing herein contained shall cease determine and be utterly void and
of none effect any thing herein contained to the contrary thereof in any wise
notwithstanding And it is hereby declared by and between the said parties that
said Andrew P. Calhoun his executors administrators or assigns do give his consent
promise and agree to and with the said Floride Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun
their executors administrators and assigns that if default shall at any time
happen to be made of in payment of the said debt or sum of money promised
according to the intent and meaning of the said bond and condition thereof
that then and in that case it shall and may be lawful to and for the said
Floride Calhoun and Cornelia L. Calhoun their executors administrators and assigns
from time to time and at all times thereafter peaceably and quietly
to enter into and upon all the aforesaid lands tenements hereditaments and premises
therein and every part thereof and to have hold possess and enjoy the same
and attain to their own use and behoof all their sum profits and advantages
from thenceforth and forever or the same to sell and dispose of at public vendue and place
