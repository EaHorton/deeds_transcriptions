The above is a true representation of a parcel of land situated in Pickens
District, S. Carolina on the East side of Coneross Creek, it being part of a tract
originally granted to Wm Allen 1785 and known as the estate of Roger & Bennett
as represented above. Containing seven acres, as taken from the original plat
of above plat, reference thereon, by the request of Mrs. E. Armstrong this 20th
day of April 1830.
Robert Fullerton D. S.
Recorded the 25th day of May 1830 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.

Know all men by these presents that
I W. L. Keith of the District of Pickens and State aforesaid for and in
consideration of the natural love and affection which I have for my Daughter Elizabeth
Jane Hester wife of W. H. Hester of Abbeville District in the state aforesaid have given
and delivered unto the said Elizabeth Jane Hester the following negroes to wit a girl about
fifteen years old named Lucy, a girl about twelve years old named Mary, a girl about
ten years old named Sarah, a girl about six years old named Rose, a boy about
four years old named Jack, and a boy about two years old named Ben, to have and to
hold the said negroes and their future increase with the said Elizabeth Jane Hester and her
heirs if her body any issue forever with the following proviso that should the said
Elizabeth Jane Hester die leaving no issue or heirs of her own body, the above named
negroes at her death are to return to me or to my heirs or assigns and I do
forever bind me and my legal representatives to warrant and defend the said negroes
unto the said Elizabeth Jane Hester and her heirs forever against all persons whomsoever
lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In witness whereof
I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 3rd day of May A. D. 1830
Signed Sealed and delivered in presence of
John D. Thomas
Thomas Gillette
State of South Carolina

I Lafayette W. Hester the husband of the within named
Elizabeth Jane Hester do hereby consent to the within deed of Gift with this condition
and proviso therein set forth and do sign the same this Eighteenth day of May 1830
Signed Sealed and acknowledged in presence of
John D. Thomas
W. H. Hester (Seal)

South Carolina Before me personally appeared S. B. Keith one of the
Pickens District who saith on oath that he saw W. L. Keith sign seal and deliver the within
instrument for the uses and purposes therein mentioned and that he with John D. Thomas
and Thomas Gillette in the presence of each other witnessed the due execution thereof.
Sworn to and subscribed before me this 18th day of May 1830
John D. Thomas
