State of South Carolina		}
Pickens District		}	Know all men by these presents that I Levi S. Robins of the State and
District aforesaid, for and in consideration of the sum of five hundred
dollars to me in hand paid by Isaac P. Pool & Pickens District South Carolina, have granted
bargained and sold unto the said Isaac P. Pool a certain negro girl slave (in which
the said Levi S. Robins holds a life time) called Nancy, aged about ten years, and also one sorrel filly, two beds
and also two beds and furniture, to have and to hold the said Nancy and
filly, beds and furniture, to the said Isaac P. Pool, his heirs and assigns forever. And I the
said Levi S. Robins, do hereby warrant and forever defend the said negro girl slave, filly, beds
and furniture, unto the said Isaac P. Pool, his heirs and assigns forever, against myself, my heirs
and assigns, and against myself, my heirs and assigns, and all other persons whomsoever.
In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 3rd day of January 1853.
Signed, sealed and delivered in presence of
Edmund Miller
State of South Carolina		}
Pickens District		}	Personally appeared Edmund Miller and
made oath that he was present and saw
L. S. Robins sign seal and deliver the within deed to I. P. Pool for the use purposes
within mentioned and that he signed the same as a witness at the request of the said
L. S. Robins and in the presence of E. J. P. Jones.
Recorded the 11th day of January 1853 and examined by W. H. Keith C.C. & M. E.
