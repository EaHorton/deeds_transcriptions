Witnesseth. That whereas I the said Thomas Alexander am desirous to make provision for my wife and
children against future contingencies and for their better maintenance and support. Now therefore
this indenture witnesseth that I the said Thomas Alexander in consideration of the premises and of
the sum of one dollar lawful money of the United States to him in hand paid by the said first par-
ties to this deed the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged have bargained and sold and in presence
of an open market delivered and by these presents do bargain and sell and enfeoff and confirm unto
the said parties of the first part all that piece or parcel of land situate lying and being in the
District and State aforesaid on the waters of a main branch of the eight mile creek. Begin at a
gum on a lateral branch by a road all and her children. Mary about 10 years old. Martha about 8
years old. Malinda about six years old. William about four years old. Also six head of horses
and one yoke of oxen and cart. To have and to hold the said piece or parcel of land and
hereditaments and singular the premises above bargained unto the aforesaid trustees aforesaid land
their heirs and assigns forever. In trust and to and for the several uses intents and purposes herein
mentioned namely. First to hold the same to and for the use of my wife Mary Alexander
during her natural life and at her death to the use of such of her children as shall be living at the
time of her death forever free from the control of any and not liable for any of my debts
hereafter contracted and with power for my said trustees to sell any of the premises to
raise any money therefrom for the support of said children that present or in any future time
the same to be held according to the provisions of this deed as if the same had herein mentioned
with reasonable compensation to the said trustees for their trouble in the sale with power of
sale to said trustees to have hold the same as above written.
have hereunto set my hand and seal this day and year above written.
Signed sealed and delivered
in presence of us
W. S. Heels
(we accept the trust)
State of South Carolina }
Pickens District } Before me personally appeared
W. S. Heels and made oath that he
was present and did see Thomas Alexander sign seal and
as and deed deliver the within deed for the uses and purposes therein mentioned
and he Heels with himself witnessed the due execution thereof sworn to
this 1st day of Octr A.D. 1853.
W. S. Heels, C.C.
Recorded the 1st day of Octr 1853 and examined by W. S. Heels, C.C. & R.M.C.
