                  Deed of Gift
The State of South Carolina  Know all men by these presents
     Pickens District  That I John Swafford of Pickens
District and State aforesaid for and in consideration of the natural
love and affection I bear and entertain for and towards my Grand Son John
M. Alexander of the same State and District aforesaid and also for and in consideration
of the sum of one dollar to me in hand paid by the said John M. Alexander at and
before the sealing and delivery hereof the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged
have given granted bargained and conveyed and by these presents do
give grant bargain sell and convey unto the said John M. Alexander
his heirs and assigns the following Slaves to wit one boy Jim eleven years
old one girl Hett eight years old one girl named Harriet Eight years of age
To have and to hold the said Slaves Jim and Harriet hereby given granted
bargained and sold unto the said John M. Alexander his heirs and assigns to the
only proper use of the said John M. Alexander his heirs and assigns forever, upon
the express condition however that it is hereby understood and agreed by and
between the said parties herein mentioned that the possession and enjoyment
of the said Slaves Jim and Harriet shall remain and continue with the said John
Swafford during his natural life. In testimony whereof I have hereunto set my
hand and seal this third day of October A. D. Eighteen hundred and fifty four
and sealed and delivered in presence of

Geo. S. Swafford
The State of South Carolina  Personally appeared before me George Swafford and made
Pickens District  oath that he was present and saw John Swafford sign
seal and deliver the within conveyance for the uses and purposes therein specified
and that he with John Swafford witnessed the due execution of the same
Sworn and subscribed before me this 7th November 1854
Recorded in the Pickens District
Recorded this 8th day of Novr 1854 and examined by W. L. Keith C. C. P. & R. M. C.

This Indenture made the twenty third
day of September in the year one thousand eight
hundred and fifty four between Joseph P. Black Junior of the one part
