1800/500g.
The State of South Carolina Whereas Coleman & Puckett and Wills
Abbeville District Puckett have this day purchased from the
Benjamin E. Posey the printing press type and all the office furniture
of every matter and kind whatsoever in connection with the office
of the paper called the Independent Press for the sum of one thousand five hundred dollars
and whereas also Coleman & Wills Puckett do own a joint interest in paying for the
same have purchased two notes from Robert E. Grier officer of said district
one for the sum of one thousand dollars and one from said Grier for the
sum of four one thousand eight hundred and fifty two signed by B. Posey
& Posey and B. Posey James H. Bolt and J. H. Ware, making at this
time the sum of two thousand one hundred and fifty two dollars and fifty cents,
which the said Coleman & Puckett and Wills Puckett have this day given their joint and several
promissory note to said Robert E. Grier with interest from date. Now know all men by these
presents that Coleman & Wills Puckett are now seized of having the prompt and certain
payment of the said last mentioned note to said Robert E. Grier and the said independents
of any and whatever the said Wills Puckett do own individually to have and to hold
the said material containing the press and the type now in use by said
Coleman & Puckett in the room of Puckett and Kitchen furniture now in use in said town
of Abbeville Court House and a negro girl slave of black color and age about fifteen
years now the property known to be the property of the said Coleman & Wills
Puckett and Wills Puckett in consideration of the premises and no further consideration of
the sum of one dollar to the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged by the said Robert
Grier have granted bargained sold and released and by these presents do grant bargain sell
and by these presents do grant bargain sell and release to & from transfer set over and deliver
unto the said Robert E. Grier his heirs and assigns forever the following property to wit one
tract of land the property of the said Wills Puckett lying in Abbeville District on waters
of Chauga Creek containing one hundred and thirty acres more or less bounded by lands of
Bay Rivered Daniel Inman and others formerly owned by General Hygo one black negro
girl named Martha about fifteen years of age the property of the said Coleman & Wills
Puckett and also the household and kitchen furniture of the said Coleman & Puckett together
with the body type the printing materials and appurtenances belonging to the office of the
paper called the Independent Press now published in Abbeville Court House (the last
item being the joint property of the said Coleman and Wills Puckett) to have and to hold
the said property for property real and personal unto the said Robert E. Grier his
heirs and assigns forever. Provided always nevertheless and it is true intent and meaning
of these presents that if the undersigned Coleman & Puckett and Wills Puckett or either of
them shall well and truly pay or cause to be paid the said note to Robert E. Grier
for $1952 with interest according to the effect thereof, then and in that
case these presents and every thing herein contained shall cease determine and be utterly void and of no effect otherwise to stand remain in full
force and virtue. In witness whereof the said Coleman & Wills Puckett
have hereunto set their hands and seals the day and year above written. Signed sealed and delivered
in the presence of us

Testimony whereof we have hereunto set our hands and affixed our seals this twenty fifth day of May in the year of our Lord one
