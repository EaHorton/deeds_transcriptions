Sheriff title for 350 acres
South Carolina. To all whom these presents shall come I Alexander Bryce
of Pickens District & now sheriff of Pickens District and State aforesaid
send Greeting. Whereas by virtue of a writ of fieri facias issued out of
the County Common Pleas held for the District of Pickens to me directed day of October
in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty one at the suit of Abraham
Jams & John C. Griffin against Jacob L. Stansell. I the said Alexander Bryce
then Sheriff the goods and chattels lands and tenements of Jacob L. Stansell
to levy did seize and take in execution and afterwards on the first Tuesday in March
the said J. L. Stansell's goods and chattels, lands and tenements of the said James
Jackson & William L. Arnold at certain houses and places in the District
aforesaid in the neighborhood of Pickens Court House the same being in the District
of Pickens and State of South Carolina the same wherein Thomas Barton now lives and
the same wherein James Jackson the now possessor doth actually possess and

title lying on Toxaway Creek adjoining lands of John Black, Jacob L. Stansell, James
Jackson and whereas the said Jacob L. Stansell did on the 13th day of October
then Sheriff made by virtue of the said writ of fieri facias levy on the said lands
to sale at public auction and purchase by Thomas N. Stansell of Pickens District
