Know all men by these presents that I Lafayette
W. Allen of Pickens District and State aforesaid for and in consideration of
the natural love good advice and affection which I the said Lafayette W. Allen have and
bear towards my little Daughter Elizabeth Luella Allen who is about three years of age and
also for and in consideration of the sum of Five dollars to me in hand paid by James
W. Earle Pickens District and State aforesaid the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged
Have granted bargained sold and delivered and by these presents do bargain sell and deliver unto the said James W.
Earle Trustee the following negro slaves viz Sarah a yellow girl about eighteen years of age
Ben a boy about fourteen years of age Jim a boy about twelve years of age and a boy
about five years of age Toog together with the increase to have and to hold the said Sarah
Jim and Toog and also the future increase of the females if there should be any unto the
said J. W. Earle in trust for the sole use benefit and enjoyment of my said Daughter
Elizabeth Allen during her natural life and after the death of such her heirs as she
may bear to be equally divided among them share and share alike but should my
Elizabeth Luella Allen leave no heirs of her own body at her death she should be a
full age if not then and in that case the said negro slaves and their increase to revert
to me. Their heirs or assigns upon the administration and becomes a part of his
estate agreeable to the meaning and intention of former and heretofore sealed and
delivered by myself. If the said Dr James W. Earle Trustee dies before said Elizabeth
Luella Allen comes of full age then and in that case the said James W. Earle
heirs or assigns shall and may have full power and authority to take the said slaves into their
possession and hold them until she comes of full age and if she dies before she comes of
full age then and in that case the said negro slaves should
be allowed to be removed from the said family of Elizabeth Allen
and that the said J. W. Earle Trustee shall have full power and authority to
employ the possession to further the interest of the said J. W. Earle Trustee and
preserve the same for the benefit of the said Elizabeth Allen and her heirs
J. W. Earle to act and do with the said negro slaves as he thinks will be most to the advantage
of the said Elizabeth Allen and the interest is hereby directed to be used for his purposes
of safety securing to and securing the said property and the interest of the said
Elizabeth Allen in every respect fully to the said James W. Earle Trustee whereof
the said Lafayette W. Allen hath hereunto set his hand and seal this eleventh day of
May in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifty four and two
seventy eighth year of American independence
Signed sealed and delivered in the presence of
John S. Earle
State of South Carolina
L. W. Allen (Seal)

State of South Carolina I James W. Earle the Trustee named in the annexed deed of trust
do hereby certify my acceptance to the same aforesaid
estate witness my hand and seal this eleventh day of May in the year of our Lord one
thousand eight hundred and fifty four In presence of
John S. Earle
State of South Carolina
J. W. Earle (Seal)

State of South Carolina Before me personally appeared John S. Earle and made oath that he saw
L. W. Allen sign seal and deliver the within deed of trust for the use and purposes mentioned and set forth and that D. W. Earle and himself
witnessed the due execution thereof. Sworn to and subscribed before me this 20th day of May 1854
John S. Earle
Recorded the 20th day of May 1854 and examined by W. L. Keith C.C.P. M.C.
