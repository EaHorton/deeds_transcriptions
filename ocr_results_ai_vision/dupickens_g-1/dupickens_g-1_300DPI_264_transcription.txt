Sheriff of Pickens District and State aforesaid send greeting. Whereas by virtue of a writ of fieri 
Facias issued out of the Court of Common Pleas for the District of Pickens tested the 
twenty sixth day of June in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifty one at the 
suit of Andrew Hunter vs. Absalom Stephens & one other, I did lately seize and levy the real 
estate of the said Absalom and commenced my advertisement of the same for sale on the 
first day of October, damages and costs thereon, and taken the lands and tenements 
of the said Absalom, to wit, all that Certain piece parcel and tract of land 
Containing one hundred and eighty acres more or less, situate and being in the 
District of Pickens State of South Carolina on waters of the waters of Twelve Mile 
River, bounded by lands of Andrew Hunter, Mary Kelley, and others, and 
whereas the said premises with their appurtenances were then exposed to public 
sale and auction at Pickens Court House on the first Monday in October, in the 
year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifty one, and the said 
property was then purchased by J. F. Doyle of the District and State aforesaid, 
the being the highest and best bidder for the highest that could be obtained, they 
have known that I, R. R. Roach, Sheriff of Pickens District, in the State aforesaid 
in consideration of the sum of one hundred and fifty five dollars to me in hand 
well and truly paid by the said J. F. Doyle at and before the sealing and delivery 
of these presents, have granted, bargained, sold and released, and by these 
presents do grant, bargain, sell and release unto the said J. F. Doyle, all that 
piece parcel and tract of land above described, together with all and singular 
the rights, members, hereditaments and appurtenances to the said premises 
belonging or in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and 
singular the premises before mentioned unto the said J. F. Doyle, his heirs and 
assigns forever. And I do hereby bind myself and my successors in office to 
warrant and forever defend all and singular the said premises unto the said 
J. F. Doyle, his heirs and assigns against myself and my successors in office 
and against every person whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim the same 
or any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this first day of October in the 
year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifty one. 
Signed, sealed and delivered in presence of 
J. F. Doyle 
R. R. Roach R. R. Roach

South Carolina } 
Pickens District } Personally appeared before me J. F. Doyle and made oath that he saw R. R. Roach, Sheriff of Pickens District 
sign, seal and deliver the within deed for the use and purposes therein mentioned, 
and that he with James G. Lewis in the presence of each other witnessed the due 
execution thereof. Sworn to before me this 1st day of 
January 1852. Thos. H. McGhee, M. P. 
Recorded the 15 day of January, 1853 and examined by W. L. Keith C. C. & R. M. C.
