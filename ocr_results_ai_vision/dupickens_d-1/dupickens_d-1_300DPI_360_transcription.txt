The State of South Carolina }
To all to whom these presents shall }
come, Know ye that I Mary G. Holland of the }
Pendleton District in the State aforesaid }
Dividends & profits now dealing: Whereas I Mary G. Holland }
of the said District & Province by my promissory note or }
notes, bearing date hereunto annexed, to these }
presents, and dated on the first day of November one thousand }
eight hundred & ten, do owe to E. B. Brown & F. Hickey for, & in consideration }
of my security for the payment thereof to the said E. B. & F. Hickey, Now Know ye }
that I Mary G. Holland, for and in consideration of the sum of one dollar }
to me in hand paid by the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, agreeable to the terms & conditions }
of the said note or obligation, have bargained, sold, assigned, transferred, & set over }
unto the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, my mortgage upon my property, to them & their }
heirs & assigns forever, to have & to hold the same, together with all my right, title, & interest }
therein, to the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their heirs & assigns forever, upon trust, nevertheless, }
that the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their heirs or assigns, shall permit me, the said Mary G. Holland, }
to remain in possession of the said property, until default shall be made in the payment of the }
said note or obligation, or any part thereof, according to the conditions thereof, and upon such }
default, it shall be lawful for the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their heirs or assigns, to sell }
the said property, or any part thereof, at public auction, for ready money, and out of the }
proceeds thereof, to pay and satisfy the said note or obligation, and all interest due thereon, }
and the surplus, if any, to pay over to me, the said Mary G. Holland, my executors, administrators, }
or assigns. And I, the said Mary G. Holland, do hereby covenant, promise, and agree to and with }
the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their heirs and assigns, that I will, at any time hereafter, }
upon the request of the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their heirs or assigns, execute and deliver }
to them, or their counsel, any further or other lawful and reasonable act or acts, thing or things, }
device or devices, in the law, for the better and more effectually assigning, transferring, and }
assuring the said property, and every part thereof, unto the said E. B. Brown & F. Hickey, their }
heirs and assigns, as by them, or their counsel, shall be reasonably devised, advised, or required. }
In witness whereof, I have hereunto set my hand and seal, this twenty-sixth day of March, in the year }
of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and ten, and in the thirty-fourth year of American Independence.
