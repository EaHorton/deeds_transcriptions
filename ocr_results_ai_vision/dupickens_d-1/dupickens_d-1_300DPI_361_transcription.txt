before me that he saw Joseph Chapman sign seal and as his act

witnesses thereto. 6th day of February 1841.
Before me W. Hutchison J. Q. S. B. Brown
Recorded the 8th day of February 1841 and examined by me
W. L. Keith C.C. & M.C.
