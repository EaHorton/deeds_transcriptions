Sworn to subscribed before me this 6th March 1823.
Ed. L. Heath C.C. & M.E.
Received the 6. day of March 1823 and examined by
Ed. L. Heath C.C. & M.E.

State of South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that I Lewis Wood
of Sumter District in the State aforesaid in consideration of the sum of
two dollars to me in hand paid at and before the sealing and delivery of these
presents by Jack Prince (freed person of color) blacksmith of the same
district and state aforesaid have bargained and sold and by these presents do
bargain and sell unto the said Jack Prince a certain negro woman named
Lucy aged about forty six years and her child named Mary aged about
four years to have and to hold the said negro woman Lucy and her child
Mary unto the said Jack Prince his executors administrators and assigns
forever and I the said Lewis Wood for myself my executors and adminis-
trators the said negro woman Lucy and her child Mary unto the said
Jack Prince his executors administrators and assigns against me the said
Lewis Wood my executors and administrators and against all and every
other person or persons whomsoever shall and will warrant and forever
defend by these presents. In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand
and seal this 6th day of March in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and twenty three and in the forty seventh year of the Independence
of the United States of America.

State of South Carolina. Personally appeared James Sanders before me and made oath
that he was present and saw Lewis Wood sign seal and as his act and deed deliver
the within conveyance for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that he with
S. D. Sumter in the presence of each other witnessed the due execution thereof.
Sworn to and subscribed before me this 6th day of
January 1823 Ed. L. Heath C.C. & M.E.

South Carolina. To all to whom these presents shall come I W. H.
Lewis Sheriff of Sumter District in the said state aforesaid send Greeting
Whereas by virtue of a Writ of Fieri Facias, issued out of the Court of Common
Pleas for the District of Sumter to me directed the suit of W. H. Lewis
against the goods and chattels lands and tenements of John Cook I have
seized and taken the right title and interest of the said John Cook of in and
to a certain tract of land containing fifty acres more or less situate lying and
being in the said District of Sumter on the waters of the Black River
bounded on the North by lands of John Cook on the East by lands of
John Cook on the South by lands of John Cook and on the West by lands
of John Cook and have advertised the same for sale on the first Monday
in March next and have sold the same to W. H. Lewis for the sum of
fifty dollars he being the highest bidder. Now know ye that I the said
W. H. Lewis Sheriff as aforesaid in consideration of the said sum of
fifty dollars to me in hand paid by the said W. H. Lewis at and before
the sealing and delivery of these presents the receipt whereof is hereby
acknowledged have granted bargained sold and released and by these
presents do grant bargain sell and release unto the said W. H. Lewis
his heirs and assigns all the right title and interest of the said John
Cook of in and to the said tract of land and premises. To have and to
hold all and singular the premises before mentioned unto the said W. H.
Lewis his heirs and assigns forever. In witness whereof I have hereunto
set my hand and seal this 6th day of March in the year of our Lord one
thousand eight hundred and twenty three and in the forty seventh year
of the Independence of the United States of America.
