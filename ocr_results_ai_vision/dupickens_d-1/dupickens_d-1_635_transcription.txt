Forty three and in the Sixty seventh year of the Independence of the United States of
America, and sealed and acknowledged in the presence of us,
Allen Fuller, A.E. Ledonne, Wm. McLellan,
Memorandum.

This day personally appeared before me, Wm. Little Smith one of the Justices of
the peace in and for Pickens District, A.E. Ledonne and being duly sworn, he saith
that he saw Jane Hamilton sign the within deed of conveyance to George W. Bopp and he then
saw Allen Fuller, Wm. McLellan subscribe their names as witnesses to the same. Sworn
to and subscribed before me this 25th day of April 1843.
Wm. Little Smith
W. L. S.
Appomattox C. H. V.
