Forty three and in the Sixty seventh year of the Independence of the United States of
America, and sealed and acknowledged in the presence of us,
Allen Fuller, A.E. Ledoux, Wm. McLellan,
Memorandum

This day personally appeared before me Winnsille Smith one of the Justices of
the peace in and for Pickens District, A.E. Ledoux and being duly sworn, saith
that he saw Jane Hamilton sign the within deed of conveyance to George W. Bopp and he then
saw Allen Fuller, Wm. McLellan subscribe their names as witnesses at the same time.
Sworn and subscribed before me this 25th day of April 1843.
Winnsille Smith
Winnsille S.C.

Know all men by these presents that I Jane
Hamilton of the state and District aforesaid
for and in consideration of the natural love and affection
which I have and bear unto George W. Bopp of the state
of Alabama and state aforesaid and also for divers other good causes and considerations
me hereunto moving, have given granted conveyed unto the said George W. Bopp his heirs and assigns forever
a certain negro woman named Mary and her child named Caroline together with
the future increase of the said negro woman Mary. To have and to hold the said negroes
unto the said George W. Bopp his heirs executors administrators forever.
I do hereby bind myself my heirs Executors and Administrators firmly by these presents that I
said George W. Bopp his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby warrant and forever
defend the title and right of the property from myself my heirs executors and administrators
and from all other persons persons whatsoever may lawfully claim the same
any part thereof. In testimony whereof I have hereunto set my hand and affixed my seal
the twentieth day of January one thousand eight hundred and forty three and in
the Sixty Seventh year of the Independence of the United States of America.
Sealed and acknowledged in the presence of us,
Allen Fuller, A.E. Ledoux, Wm. McLellan,
Memorandum

This day personally appeared before me William Smith one of the Justices
of the peace in and for Pickens District, A.E. Ledoux and being duly sworn, saith
that he saw Jane Hamilton sign the within deed of conveyance and he saw Allen
Fuller, Wm. McLellan subscribe their names as witnesses at the same time. Sworn and subscribed
before me this 25th day of April 1843. Winnsille S.C.
Winnsille S.C.
Recorded the 1st day of May 1843 and examined by
let H. Steele C.C. & M. E.
