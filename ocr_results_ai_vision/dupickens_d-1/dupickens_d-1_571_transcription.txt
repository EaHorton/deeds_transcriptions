and deliver and by these presents bargain sell and confirm unto the 
said W. H. Heath and W. H. Laddett Trustees the 
following slaves to wit. Charlotte about thirty five years of age and her five 
children viz Jack about twenty one years of age, Henretta about fifteen years 
old, Mary about thirteen years old, Thomas about nine years old, and Maria 
about seven years old, also Sarah about thirty five years of age and her three 
children to wit Ellen about seven years old Henry about five years old and 
Hannah about one year old, Abram about forty years old, Allen about fifteen 
years old, together with all my stock of Horses, Cattle, Sheep and Hogs farming 
utensils, household and kitchen furniture together with the Crop of Corn, Cotton 
and whatever else may be on the premises. To have and to hold all and singular 
the said slaves together with the future increase of the females thereof, stock, 
tools and property aforesaid to the said W. H. Heath and W. H. Laddett Trustees 
their heirs and assigns forever for the only proper use benefit and behoof of the 
said Elisha Lawrence his heirs and assigns forever. And the said Elisha Law- 
rence doth covenant to and with the said W. H. Heath and W. H. Laddett Trustees 
their heirs and assigns that he the said Elisha Lawrence is the true and lawful 
owner of the above property and hath full power to dispose of the same and that 
the same is free from all incumbrances and that he will warrant and defend the 
same to the said W. H. Heath and W. H. Laddett Trustees their heirs and assigns 
forever against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever. In witness whereof 
I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 5th day of November in the year of our 
Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two and in the sixty seventh year of 
the Sovereignty and Independence of the United States of America.

State of South Carolina, Kershaw District } 
Personally appeared before me Wm. Oliver and made oath that he saw 
Elisha Lawrence sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within 
deed of trust to be used by way of a mortgage of the personal property 
therein mentioned for the purposes therein expressed and that he with 
John A. Sheddan in the presence of each other witnessed the due execution 
thereof. Sworn to before me this 5th day of November in the year of our 
Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two. 

State of South Carolina, Kershaw District } 
I Wm. Oliver Notary Public and Ex Officio Justice of the Quorum do hereby 
certify unto all whom it may concern that John A. Sheddan whose name 
appears to the foregoing deed of trust as a subscribing witness is a 
respectable person and to be believed on oath. Given under my hand and 
seal this 5th day of November in the year of our Lord one thousand eight 
hundred and forty two. 

State of South Carolina, Kershaw District }

therein mentioned for the purposes therein expressed and that he with 
John A. Sheddan in the presence of each other witnessed the due execution 
thereof. Sworn to before me this 5th day of November in the year of our
