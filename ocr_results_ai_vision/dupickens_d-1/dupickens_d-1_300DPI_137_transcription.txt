The State of South Carolina 
Know all men by these presents that I Allen 
Knight of Pickens District in the State aforesaid for and in consideration 
of the sum of fifteen dollars to me in hand paid by Benjamin Logan 
late of Pickens, Have granted, bargained, sold and released, and by these presents do grant, 
bargain sell and release, unto William D. Steele all that piece or parcel of land containing 
said fifteen acres, more or less, being a part of the land on which James Edgerton formerly 
lived, situate in the District aforesaid, on a branch of Twelve Mile River, waters of Keowee, 
bounded by lands of John Edgerton, and others, being a part of the land granted to 
W. H. Steele by grant of 831 acres dated 30 July 1787. Whereon W. H. & C. a small part of 204 
acres (part of 831) known as Lot No. 8, John Edgerton's station, with the said fifteen acres, included therein 
at the beginning corner. Together with all and singular the rights members hereditaments 
and appurtenances to the said premises belonging or in any wise incident or appertaining. 
To have and to hold all and singular the premises before mentioned unto the said 
William D. Steele his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind myself my heirs 
executors and administrators to warrant and forever defend all and singular the said 
premises unto the said William D. Steele his heirs and assigns against myself and my 
heirs and against all manner of persons lawfully claiming or to claim the same or 
any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this 8th day of May in the year of our 
Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty nine and in the sixty third year of the sovereignty 
and independence of the United States of America. 
Signed sealed and delivered in the presence of us 
Elijah E. Alexander, Joseph Brown

South Carolina: Before me personally came Elijah E. Alexander and made oath that he was 
present and saw Allen Knight sign (by making his mark) seal and as his act and deed 
deliver the within deed of conveyance for the uses and purposes therein mentioned and that he with 
Joseph Brown in presence of each other witnessed the due execution thereof. Sworn to before me 
this 8th day of May 1839. 
Elijah E. Alexander 
Recorded the 8th day of May 1839 and examined by me
