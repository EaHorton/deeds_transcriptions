The State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents 
that I John Couch of the District of Anderson and State aforesaid in 
consideration of the sum of five dollars to me in hand paid by Alexander Orr of Anderson District do this present 
time in the maintenance and support of my daughter the Lucinda Henry during the 
life of her mother Nancy Henry given and granted assigned and delivered and by 
these presents do convey assign and deliver unto the said Alexander Orr one 
negro girl slave named Lydia and her two children one called Matilda two 
years old and the other a female child two weeks old. Also two feather beds two bed steads and furniture, 
also one cow and calf. To have and to hold the said three female slaves and their 
increase unto the said Alexander Orr for the use and trust for the said Lucinda 
Henry during her natural life and in case she dies leaving no children born of her body then living, then to such child or 
children as she may leave living at her death to have and to hold forever. But in case 
the Lucinda Henry should die without issue of her body living then and 
in that case the said slaves and their increase and the other property sold 
and become a part thereof to be distributed between or among my heirs as 
fully and entirely as if the property of the said negroes and other property 
by these letters of trust had never been made. Witness my hand and seal this 28th day of December 
1842. Signed sealed and acknowledged and delivered in presence of 
J. H. Sloan (Seal) 
W. L. Keith C. C. P. M. C.
