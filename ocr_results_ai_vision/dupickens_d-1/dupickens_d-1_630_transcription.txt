Recieved the 8th day of April 1843 and examined by Wm. L. Steele C.C. & R.M.C.

South Carolina } I John Venner of the state and district aforesaid
Richland District } being feeble and weak in body though of a
sound mind and memory, do will and dispose of my plantation property in the
following manner (that is to say) to my son David G.
Venner I give my Negro woman Sarah and girl Clara, and to my
daughter Rebecca Venner I give my Negro woman Nance her two children Moses
& Martha and my Negro boy Tom. To my son Samuel I Venner I give my Negro
boy Jack, to my son Caleb & Horace I Venner I give my Negro woman Fanny my Negro
boy Frank and Negro girl Shealey. And to my son Ebenezer P. Venner I give my
Negro boy Sam and my interest or claim in the store that is situated in the first
lane of the city of Charleston S.C. To my daughter Caroline I Venner I give my
Negro girl Mary Ann and Mary Ann's boy child Sam. I wish it hereby
understood that my Negroes are not to be sold or disposed of until after the death
of my wife Rebecca, these Negroes (Sarah) and those in the last named clause
are to be at the service of my wife during her life. I also give my wife
Rebecca all my household furniture, beds, bedsteads, bedding, and every
thing that has heretofore been in service amongst them. In testimony hereof I have hereunto set
my hand and seal the 19th day of January in the year of our Lord 1843, and in the 67th
year of the Independence of America.
John Venner (Seal)
Signed, Sealed and
delivered in presence of
Martin Venner
South Carolina } Personally appeared before me Martin Venner and
Richland District } Polson Outlaw, and made oath that we saw John Venner sign, seal and deliver the
within deed of Gift for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that we believe
him to be of sound mind and memory. Sworn to before me this 19th day of January 1843.
Wm. L. Steele
Martin Venner
Polson Outlaw
Recorded the 8th day of April 1843 and examined by Wm. L. Steele C.C. & R.M.C.

South Carolina } This Indenture made by and between Nathaniel
Richland District } Merritt of the one part and Temperance M. Martin of the
aforesaid parish in consideration of the sum of one dollar
to him in hand paid by the said Temperance M. Martin at and before the sealing
and delivery thereof, the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged, hath granted, bargained, sold and
released, and by these presents doth grant, bargain, sell and release unto the said Temperance M. Martin
all that piece parcel or tract of land containing seventy five and a half acres, situate
in Richland District and bounded on the north by Doctor Woods, the east by
a part of land originally granted to Robert Anderson, south by James Kelly 1787,
and west by land of James McCants. To have and to hold all and singular the
premises before mentioned unto the said Temperance M. Martin, her heirs and
assigns forever. In witness whereof the said Nathaniel Merritt hath hereunto set
his hand and seal this the eighth day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and forty three, and in the sixty seventh year of the Independence of the United States of America.
Nathaniel Merritt (Seal)
