on a subscribing witness with himself, sworn to before me this 6th April 1843
Wm. L. Steele
Recieved the 8th day of April 1843 and examined by W. L. Steele C.C. & M.E.

South Carolina } I John Venner of the state and District aforesaid
Richland District } being feeble and weak in body though of sound
mind and memory, have deemed it necessary for the disposal of my perishable property in the
manner and form following, that is to say, to my son David G.
Venner I give my Negro woman Sarah and Girl Clara, and to my
Daughter Rebecca Venner I give my Negro woman Maria her two children Moses
& Martha and my Negro boy Tom. To my son Samuel I Venner I give my Negro
boy John and my Coal black Horse Charley. To my son Francis W. Venner I give my Negro
boy Frank and Negro Girl Shealey. And to my son Ebenezer P. Venner I give my
Negro boy Sam and my interest or claim in the Store that is situated in the Town of
Camden and to my son James G. P. Venner I give my Daughter Rebecca Venner
Venner I give my Negro woman Mary and her boy child Sam. I wish & hereby
direct that my Negro boy George be sold and the proceeds divided upon the said
property to be divided equally between her Mother and her Sisters, Rebecca, Maria
and my son Ebenezer, three Negroes (Sarah) Maria and her two children and my wife
Rebecca Venner during her life or widowhood, and at her death I wish my wife
think best, and further in regard of my Books, Plantation tools, and farming
utensils, I wish my Executors to dispose of them at publick auction, after giving
reasonable notice, and after paying all my just debts, I wish the proceeds to be
equally divided between my three sons Francis, Ebenezer and James G. P. Venner
and my three children that have heretofore been advanced amongst them. In testimony hereof I have hereunto set
my hand and seal this 19th day of January in the year of our Lord 1843, and in the 67
year of American Independence.
John Venner (Seal)
Signed, Sealed and
delivered in presence of us
South Carolina } Personally appeared before me Martin Venner and
Richland District } Polson Outlaw, and made oath that we saw John Venner sign, seal and deliver the
within deed of Gift for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that we believe
him to be of sound mind and memory. Sworn to before me this 19th day of January 1843.
Wm. L. Steele
Magistrate
Recorded the 8th day of April 1843 and examined by W. L. Steele C.C. & M.E.

South Carolina } This Indenture made by and between Nathaniel
Richland District } Merritt of the one part and Temperance M. Martin of the other
part, witnesseth that for and in consideration of the sum of one hundred
dollars to me in hand paid at and before the sealing and delivery of these presents, the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged, have given and granted and by these presents do
give and grant unto the said Temperance M. Martin and her heirs, their
assigns, all that piece parcel or tract of land containing seventy five, and a half acres, situate
in Richland District and State aforesaid, on the waters of Doctor's Creek the same being
part of a tract of land originally granted to Robert Anderson 19th of January 1787.
To have and to hold the said premises unto the said Temperance M. Martin, her heirs and
the said Nathaniel Merritt, his heirs and assigns, forever. In witness whereof I have hereunto
set my hand and seal this the eighth day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand
eight hundred and forty three, and of American Independence the sixty seventh.
Signed, Sealed and delivered in presence of us.
