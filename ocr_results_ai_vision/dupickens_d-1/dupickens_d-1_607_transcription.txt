Sworn to and subscribed the 25th day of Augt 1842
J. H. Boston N.P.R.
Recorded the 25th day of February 1843 and examined by
W. L. Keith C.C. & M.E.

State of South Carolina. Know all men by these presents, that I
W. L. Keith of Prince District and State aforesaid, for and in consideration
of the sum of two thousand and fifty four Dollars ($2054) to me in hand paid by James Hansell of the same place have granted,
bargained, sold and released, and by these presents do grant, bargain and sell
said James Hansell all that plantation or tract of land, situate, lying and being
situate in Prince District and State aforesaid, on Cypress Creek, on the East fork of Little
Saluda River, adjoining lands of Elisha L. Dyer, James Dyer, and others, containing
two hundred and twenty two acres more or less, being the same tract of land
to the original paper now fully described, and fully clear and free of expense
by J. H. Boston from James Hansell. Reference to said Deed is fully made. Together with
the rights, members, hereditaments and appurtenances to the said lands belonging or
in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the
premises unto the said James Hansell his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby
bind myself, my heirs, executors, and administrators to warrant and forever defend
all and singular the said lands unto the said James Hansell his heirs and assigns
against me, my heirs, and all persons whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim
the same or any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this twenty fifth day of Octr 1842
and in the sixty seventh year of the Independence of the United States of America.
Signed, Sealed and delivered in presence of
John Prator
E. H. Lipscomb

State of South Carolina. Personally appeared John Prator one of the within
witnesses and made oath that he saw W. L. Keith sign, seal and deliver the within convey-
ance as his act and deed for the uses and purposes within mentioned and E. H.
Lipscomb with himself witnessed the same. Sworn to and subscribed this 25th day of Octr 1842
before me W. L. Keith C.C.
John Prator

Know all men by these presents, that I
John Prator of the County of Benton and
State of Alabama, for the consideration of the natural love and af-
fection I have for my daughter, Margaret Hunter, late of
South Carolina & Robert Hunter and the further consideration of three hundred dollars
to me in hand paid, do give, grant and convey unto the said Margaret Hunter and
to her bodily heirs forever, the named negroes, to wit: one negro girl named Phebe and
increase, a negro boy named Sam. To have and to hold the said negroes to the aforesaid
said John Hunter & the heirs of her body forever, for the use and benefit of the said
benefit of the said Margaret & her bodily heirs forever, the said negroes to have and
to hold and use and occupy the said property, in the year of our Lord one thousand
eight hundred and forty two, the 21st day of Janry. In witness whereof I have hereunto
set my hand and seal this 21st day of Janry, in the year of our Lord one thousand
eight hundred and forty two.
John Prator

Before me personally appeared John Prator, who after being duly
sworn, deposeth and saith that he saw John Prator sign, seal and deliver
the within deed of gift to Margaret Hunter for the uses and purposes as therein expressed
and that he with E. H. Lipscomb the named witnesses the same. Sworn to and subscribed
