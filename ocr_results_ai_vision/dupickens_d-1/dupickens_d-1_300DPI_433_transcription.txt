The State of South Carolina
Know all men by these presents
That I Amos G. Robinson of Pendleton District in the State
aforesaid in consideration of the sum of Two Thousand
and eight hundred Dollars to me in hand paid by James Overton Lewis of the same
District in Pendleton District in the State aforesaid. I have granted bargained
bargained, sold and released and by these presents do grant bargain, sell and release
unto the said James Overton Lewis all that piece or parcel of land containing
seventeen hundred and seventy acres in the county of Pendleton on a
branch of Seneca, called Seneca River, on the waters of the said Seneca River on a
resurvey by Samuel McAdams, formerly containing one thousand and thirty one acres
more or less to me by Simeon Theus on the 26th day of May 1823. The Conveyance
Recorded in the Office of Mesne Conveyance in Pendleton District in Book O.
Page 252. The other land originally granted to the Reverend Henry Purcell Esquire
the 21st of January 1785. In the said Record in the Secretary Office of State in
Page 111. Sold by Thomas Wood Esquire Commissioner in Equity by Order of the said Court
as the property of the said Simeon Theus conveyed to me by the said Thomas Wood
containing four hundred and fifty acres the Conveyance in the Office and Court Register
Office of Mesne Conveyance in Pendleton District Book P page 228. 29 the two
Grants lying adjoining to each other in York County on the waters of Seneca
Originally granted, more on that part of Pendleton called Pickens County, containing
one thousand seven hundred & seventy acres more or less. Together with all and singular
the rights members and appurtenances to the said premises belonging or in anywise
incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the
premises before mentioned unto the said James Overton Lewis his heirs and
And assigns forever. And I do hereby bind myself my Executors and Administrators to warrant
and forever defend all and singular the said premises unto the said James Overton Lewis
his heirs and assigns against myself and my heirs and against all manner of persons
whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof.
Witness my hand and seal this twenty eighth day in the year of our Lord one
thousand eight hundred and twenty eight and in the fifty second year of the
Independence of the United States of America.
Signed sealed and delivered in the presence of
Wm. West & Francis Beek
Amos G. Robinson (Seal)
