State of South Carolina   Know all men by these presents
Pendleton District        that I William Kirksey of the
District aforesaid and state aforesaid for and in consideration
of the love and affection which I have for and bear to my
son Sam Kirksey of the District and State aforesaid and
for 5 Dollars to me in hand paid by Sam Kirksey of Anderson District & State aforesaid
the receipt and payment whereof is hereby acknowledged, I have bargained delivered
and by these presents do bargain deliver and convey unto the said Sam Kirksey in trust
with the said Kirksey trustee the following slaves (to wit) Melinda a Girl about
fifteen years of age to have and to hold the said Melinda to the said Sam Kirksey
trustee his heirs and assigns forever in trust for the use of H. Williams his
heirs and assigns forever. I do warrant and forever defend the right and title
to the said Melinda against myself my heirs executors and administrators and
against the lawful claim or claims of any other person or persons whomsoever
unto the said Sam Kirksey trustee his heirs executors and administrators and
assigns forever. Witness my hand and seal this twenty first day of December in the
year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty one and in the sixty six year of American
Independence. Signed sealed and delivered in the presence of
Christopher Kirksey, C. H. Kirksey
Wm Kirksey (Seal)
State of South Carolina   Know all men by these presents that I Sam Kirksey the
Pendleton District        trustee in the within deed do hereby certify that I have received
from H. Williams of the said district aforesaid, three hundred and forty
dollars this fifteenth day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and forty two.
Sam Kirksey (Seal)
