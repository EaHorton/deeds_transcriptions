State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents
Fair Kirksey in trust that I William Kirksey of the
for A. Williams District of Pickens and State aforesaid for and in consideration
of the love and affection which I have for and bear to my
son Fair Kirksey of the District and State aforesaid and
of five Dollars to me in hand paid by Fair Kirksey of Pickens District & State aforesaid
the receipt and payment whereof is hereby acknowledged, I have bargained delivered
and by these presents do bargain deliver and confirm unto the said Fair Kirksey
with the said Fair Kirksey trustee for the following slaves (to wit) Melinda a Girl about
fourteen years of age to have and to hold the said Melinda to the said Fair Kirksey
his heirs and assigns to the only proper use and benefit of the said Fair Kirksey his
heirs and assigns forever. And I the said William Kirksey for myself my heirs
and assigns do hereby covenant and agree to and with the said Fair Kirksey his
heirs and assigns that I am the lawful owner of the said Melinda and have
a good right and title to the said Melinda and that I will warrant and
defend the said Melinda to the said Fair Kirksey his heirs and assigns against
the lawful claims and demands of all persons whomsoever. In witness whereof
I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 7th day of March in the year of our
Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two and in the sixty sixth year of American
Independence.
Christopher Kirksey, C. E. Kirksey Wm Kirksey (Seal)
State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents that I Fair Kirksey the
aforesaid District of Pickens and State aforesaid for and in consideration of the
love and affection which I have for and bear to my son A. Williams and
of five Dollars to me in hand paid by the said A. Williams the receipt and payment
whereof is hereby acknowledged have bargained delivered and by these presents do
bargain deliver and confirm unto the said A. Williams the following slaves (to wit)
Melinda a Girl about fourteen years of age to have and to hold the said Melinda
to the said A. Williams his heirs and assigns to the only proper use and benefit of the
said A. Williams his heirs and assigns forever. And I the said Fair Kirksey for myself
my heirs and assigns do hereby covenant and agree to and with the said A. Williams
his heirs and assigns that I am the lawful owner of the said Melinda and have
a good right and title to the said Melinda and that I will warrant and
defend the said Melinda to the said A. Williams his heirs and assigns against
the lawful claims and demands of all persons whomsoever. In witness whereof
I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 7th day of March in the year of our
Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two and in the sixty sixth year of American
Independence.
Christopher Kirksey, C. E. Kirksey Fair Kirksey (Seal)
State of South Carolina Personally appeared before me C. E. Kirksey and made
Pickens District oath that he saw William Kirksey sign seal and deliver the within
deed of trust for the use and purpose therein mentioned and that he with
Christopher Kirksey and Fair Kirksey witnessed the due execution thereof.
Sworn to and subscribed before me this 7th day of March 1842.
Geo. A. P. Day C. E. Kirksey
