Georgia }
Oglethorpe Cty } Personally appeared before me, Mason Read who being
duly sworn deposeth and saith that Elizabeth Colby is now
a free woman, and that she was born free and never has been, and that her
mother was a free woman, and that he has known the said Elizabeth
Colby to have been together, free and undisturbed as before me this 16th of January
1813. and sworn before me
Christian Price Justice

State of Georgia }
Oglethorpe County } This is to certify that Elizabeth Colby is a free woman
and has been so from her nativity, and is now in the enjoyment of the blessings
of freedom in this place. I have known her from her cradle, and have
always seen her in the full enjoyment of every civil liberty, that is, which is
granted to any free person of color. August 1st 1812.

I do hereby certify that the above mentioned Elizabeth Colby, I have her time
and liberty to leave or that she is now free. August 1st 1812. John Thomson J.P.
