The State of South Carolina  }  I William D. Steele one of the Magistrates for Pickens
Pickens District          }  District do hereby certify unto all whom it may
concern that Lewis Rice the wife of the within named Steven Rice did this
day appear before me and upon being by me privately & separately examined did
declare that she does freely, voluntarily & without any compulsion dread or
fear of any person or persons whomsoever renounce release and forever relinquish
unto the within named Joel Kelly his heirs and assigns all her interest & estate and
also all her right and claim of Dower of in or to all and singular the premises
within mentioned and released. Given under my hand and seal this 26th day of January A.D. 1842.
W. D. Steele M. P.  (Seal)

Deed of Trust.
State of South Carolina  }  Know all men by these presents
Pickens District        }  that I Elisha Lawrence of the
State and District aforesaid for and in consideration of
the love and affection which I have towards my wife
Martha Lawrence and in consideration of the sum of one dollar to me in hand
paid by W. L. Keith and G. W. Liddell Trustees of the State and District aforesaid
the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged, have granted, bargained, sold and
released unto the said W. L. Keith & G. W. Liddell Trustees as aforesaid all and
singular my real estate consisting of two tracts
of land lying and being in the District and State aforesaid, one tract containing one hundred
and fifty acres on the East side of Keowee River on the State and District aforesaid, adjoining
lands of James Lawrence on the South and lands of Dr. J. B. Earle on the North and
East and on the West by lands of Joel Kelly and others and the other tract containing
one hundred and fifty acres on the West side of Keowee River on the State and District
aforesaid adjoining lands of James Lawrence on the South and lands of Dr. J. B. Earle on
the North and East and on the West by lands of Joel Kelly and others. Together with
all and singular the rights, members, hereditaments and appurtenances to the said
premises belonging or in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold
all and singular the said premises unto the said W. L. Keith and G. W. Liddell as
Trustees as aforesaid, their heirs and assigns forever for the uses and purposes
herein mentioned, that is to say for the sole use and benefit of the heirs of Martha
Lawrence during her natural life and after her death to the use of the said heirs
forever. And I do hereby bind myself my heirs, executors and
administrators to warrant and forever defend all and singular the said premises
unto the said W. L. Keith and G. W. Liddell Trustees as aforesaid their heirs and
assigns against myself and my heirs and against every person whomsoever lawfully
claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In witness whereof I have hereunto
set my hand and seal this the 1st day of November A.D. one thousand eight hundred
and forty two and in the sixty seventh year of the Independence of the United States
of America.
