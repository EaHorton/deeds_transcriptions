Know all men by these presents that I Dovia Barton of the State of South
Carolina & Pickens District, in consideration of the natural love and affec-
tion which I have and bear to my Daughter Mary I. Haskin and for divers other
good causes and considerations, especially moving have given, let me assign by these
presents do give, grant, assign and confirm unto the said Mary I. Haskin
one certain tract or track of land containing fifteen acres lying and being in the said
State & District that I bought of Jos. Haskin and joining my own land on the East and
land of John Haskin on the West and I do give unto her the above property to her and her
heirs forever. In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 5th day of July A.D. 1841.

Personally appeared before me Thos W. Haskin
and made oath that he saw Dovia Barton sign and deliver the within deed of
Gift for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that Henry Haskin and Jacob
Haskin was subscribing witnesses with himself. Sworn to before me this 7th day of March
1842. W. H. D. Gaillard

South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that I Jaban
Mauldin of Pickens District & State aforesaid in consideration of the sum of
four hundred and thirty eight dollars to me in hand paid by Moseley Mauldin all
that well parcel of land situate lying in Pickens District and State of South
Carolina, containing eighty acres more or less beginning on a pine
on John Childers line thence N 70 W 20 to a stake on the branch thence down said branch
to a stake at Black Jack & S 30 W 7 to a conditional corner thence nearly east to a sourwood
near the branch thence South east to a Rock corner thence South east to a dogwood
thence nearly east to a white oak near a gap of a fence thence along the gap to a stake
on a line of fence thence along the fence nearly east to John Childers line thence
along said line to the beginning Together with all and singular the rights
members hereditaments and appurtenances thereunto belonging or in anywise
incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the said premises unto
the said Moseley Mauldin his heirs Executors administrators or assigns forever. And
I do hereby bind myself my heirs Executors administrators and assigns forever and
against any person or persons lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In testimony whereof I have
hereunto set my hand and seal this 10th day of August eighteen hundred and forty
one. Signed sealed and delivered in presence of us

Personally came John Lesly before me the subscribing Magistrate and
made oath that he saw the within deed of conveyance signed sealed and delivered
by Jaban Mauldin for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that Right Childers with
himself was a subscribing witness to the due execution of the same.
