Know all men by these presents that I Dovia Barton of the State of South
Carolina & Marion District, in consideration of the natural love and affec-
tion which I have and bear for my Daughter Mary Haskin and for divers other
good causes and considerations, especially moving have given, let me to give by these
presents do give and grant unto the said Mary my negro girl (Sarah) forever
to have and to hold the said negro girl Sarah unto the said Mary her heirs and
assigns forever. And I the said Dovia Barton do warrant and forever defend the
said negro girl Sarah unto the said Mary her heirs and assigns against myself
and my heirs and against every other person or persons whomsoever claiming
or to claim the same or any part thereof. In testimony whereof I have hereunto set
my hand and seal this 5 day of July A.D. 1841.

Personally appeared before me Thos W. Haskin
and made oath that he saw Dovia Barton sign seal and deliver the within deed of
Gift for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that Henry Haskin and Sarah
W. Haskin was subscribing witnesses with himself. Sworn to before me this 21st day of March
1842 Wm. E. Martin

South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that I Jaban
Mauldin of Marion District, in consideration of the natural love and affection
I have for my son Moseley Mauldin and for divers other good causes and
considerations me hereunto moving have given and granted and by these presents, do
grant bargain sell and release for and in consideration of the sum
of Two hundred and thirty eight dollars to me in hand paid by Moseley Mauldin all
that well parcel of land situate in Pickens District on waters of Twelve
Mile Creek, State of South Carolina, containing eighty acres more or less beginning on a pine
on lands of John Childress lower line thence N. 30 E. 20 to a stake thence S. 60 E. 20 to a
corner on a black jack S. 30 W. 27 to a conditional corner thence nearly east to a corner
near the branch thence south east to a rock corner thence south east to a dogwood
thence nearly east to a stake corner on a post oak, thence along the old line to a stake
on a post oak corner thence along the aforesaid nearly east to John Childress line thence
along said line to the beginning. Together with all and singular the rights
members hereditaments and appurtenances thereunto belonging or in anywise
incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the said premises unto
the said Moseley Mauldin his heirs Executors Administrators or assigns forever. And
I do hereby bind myself my heirs Executors Administrators and assigns forever and
warrant and forever defend the said premises unto the said Moseley Mauldin his heirs
assigns or any person or persons lawfully
claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In testimony whereof I have
hereunto set my hand and seal this 10th day of August eighteen hundred and forty
one. Forty six years of the Independence of the United States of
America. Signed sealed and delivered in presence of us

Personally came before me John Lesly before me the subscribing Magistrate and
made oath that he saw Jaban Mauldin sign seal and deliver the within deed of
conveyance for the use and purposes therein mentioned and that he with George and Delaney
Hamilton in the presence of each other did witness the due execution of the same.
