Know all men by these presents that I Robert Vale of the State  
of Maryland Baltimore County have this day bargained sold  
and delivered a Negro Slave in fee by the name of Eliza  
unto Rosannah Beavice of the State of South Carolina, Pend-  
leton District for the sum of Two hundred & fifty dollars, current funds  
and I warrant this slave sound & healthy I also warrant the right of said  
slave unto Rosannah Beavice and her heirs forever and I do forever  
myself my heirs or assigns any other person or persons laying any legal process or  
claim to this slave witness my hand and seal this 10th day of October 1830.  

Test. Abraham Beavice, Friend & brother  
Robert Vale (Seal)  
South Carolina Pendleton County came Abraham Beavice before me the subscribing  
Justice & declared on oath that he was present & saw that the said Robert  
Vale sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within Bill of Sale to Rosannah  
Beavice for the use and purpose of its mention. Given under my hand and seal  
subscribed before me this 12th of October 1830.  
Abraham Beavice  
John Stevenson N.P.  

South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that  
I Eli Cleveland of the State and  
District aforesaid for and in consideration of the sum of  
Fourteen hundred dollars to me in hand paid by J. P. Reeder of  
the State and District aforesaid the receipt whereof I hereby acknowledge  
have granted bargained and sold and conveyed unto J. P. Reeder of the State  
and District aforesaid all that tract or parcel of land containing one hundred  
& Twelve acres more or less situate in the State & District aforesaid on the branches  
of Tugalo River adjoining lands of the Jefferson heirs, John Cleveland  
Benjamin Hearne and James Richards, Together with all the singular the  
premises above mentioned unto the said J. P. Reeder his heirs and assigns  
forever, And do bind myself my heirs and assigns to warrant and forever  
warrant and forever defend the above mentioned premises unto J. P. Reeder his  
heirs and assigns forever from myself my heirs and assigns and from all and  
all other persons lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. In testi-  
mony of which I hereunto set my hand and affixed my seal this 26th Septem-  
ber in the eight hundred and thirty year of our Lord and in the fifty fifth year of  
American independence.  
Eli Cleveland (Seal)  

South Carolina. Personally appeared John C.  
Blair before me and made oath that he saw Eli  
Cleveland sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within deed to J. P. Reeder  
for the use and purpose of its within mentioned and that Bennett Hill & Henry  
Cleveland with himself in the presence of each other witness the same. Sworn  
this 26th day of Sept 1830.  
John C. Blair  
Magistrate N.P.
