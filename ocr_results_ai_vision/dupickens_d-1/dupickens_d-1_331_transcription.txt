                        259
Know all men by these presents that I Robert Vale of the State
Maryland Baltimore County have this day bargained sold
and delivered a Negro Slave in the age of Man by the
name of Rosannah Beavice of the State of South Carolina
aforesaid for the sum of Two hundred & fifty dollars, current money
and I warrant this said servant Healthy I also warrant the right of property
in the said Rosannah Beavice unto the said Rosannah Beavice her heirs
assigns my heirs or assigns any other person forever by these presents to refuse
claim to this or interest in and to said slave this 10th day of October 1830
                Robert Vale (seal)
Test. Abraham Beavice, Friend for him
South Carolina Personally came Abraham Beavice before me the subscribing
Robert Vale (seal) Justice and made oath in due form of law that he saw Robert
Vale sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within Deed to Rosannah
Beavice for the purposes in the within Deed mentioned and that he the said
Abraham Beavice with the other witness to the said Deed and for him as a
subscribing before me 12th of October 1830
                Abraham Beavice
                John Stevenson N.P.
Recorded the 11th day of October 1830 and examined by me
                W. L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.

South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that
                I Eli Cleveland of the State and
                District aforesaid for and in consideration of the sum of
                Fourteen hundred dollars to me in hand paid by J. P. Reeder of
the State and District aforesaid the receipt whereof I hereby acknowledge
have granted bargained and sold and conveyed unto J. P. Reeder of the State
and District aforesaid all that tract or parcel of land containing one hundred
& fourteen acres more or less situate in the State District aforesaid on the branches
of Tugalo River adjoining lands of the Jefferson heirs, John Cleveland
Benjamin Hearst and James Richards, Together with all the singular the
premises above mentioned unto the said J. P. Reeder his heirs and assigns
forever, And do bind myself my heirs and assigns to warrant and forever defend
the above mentioned premises to the said J. P. Reeder his
heirs and assigns forever against myself my heirs and assigns and against all
other persons lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof, In testi-
mony whereof I have hereunto set my hand and affixed my seal this 26th Septem-
ber in the eight hundred and thirty nine
                signed sealed and delivered in presence of
                John C. Blair
                Robert Quincey
                Eli Cleveland (seal)
State of South Carolina. Personally appeared John C.
                Blair before me and made oath that he saw Eli
Cleveland sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within Deed to J. P. Reeder
for the uses and purposes in within mentioned and that Bennett Hill is a
subscribing witness to the same. Sworn to before me this 26th day of Sep-
tember 1839
                John C. Blair
                Myers N.P.
Recorded the 11th day of October 1830 and examined by me
                W. L. Keith C.C. & R.M.C.
