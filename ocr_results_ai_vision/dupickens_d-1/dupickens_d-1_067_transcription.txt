State of Indiana }
Harrison County }  ss.  This indenture witnesseth that Henry
Vance of the State and County aforesaid for and
in consideration of the love and affection I bear to my beloved son
in law Richard Kephart and his wife and for the further con-
sideration of one dollar to me in hand paid the receipt whereof is hereby
acknowledged have given granted and bargained and sold and by these presents do give
grant bargain and sell unto the said Richard Kephart and his wife and their heirs
and assigns forever the following described real estate (to wit) the west half of the
south east quarter of section twenty six (26) in township number four (4) south of range
number three (3) east in the district of lands subject to sale at Jeffersonville Indiana
containing eighty acres more or less to have and to hold the said premises with all
the appurtenances thereunto belonging to the said Richard Kephart and his wife and
their heirs and assigns forever and I the said Henry Vance for myself my heirs executors
and administrators do covenant and agree to and with the said Richard Kephart and his
wife and their heirs and assigns that I am lawfully seized in fee of the premises afore-
said that they are free from all incumbrances and that I will warrant and defend the
same against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever. In testimony whereof I have
hereunto set my hand and seal this twenty fifth day of April in the year one thousand
eight hundred and thirty eight.
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of us Joseph B. Thompson
Henry Vance (seal)
State of Indiana }
Harrison County } ss.  Before me the undersigned a Justice of the
Peace in and for said County personally appeared Henry Vance and acknowledged the
execution of the foregoing deed for the purposes therein mentioned. Given under my hand
and seal this 25th day of April 1838.
Daniel J. Thompson
Justice of the Peace (seal)
Recorded the 22nd day of October 1838 as examined by
L. S. Heth C.C. H.C.M.C.
