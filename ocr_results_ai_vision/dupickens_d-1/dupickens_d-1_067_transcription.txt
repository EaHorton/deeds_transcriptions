State of Alabama }
Jackson County } Know all men by these presents that I Henry
in consideration of the love and affection I bear to my beloved son
Daniel Kephart or Hephburn and for the further consideration of the
good causes hereunto moving me, do by these presents give bargain sell and transfer unto
my beloved daughter before marriage gave give and transfer unto my said son his
executors heirs and assigns the largest bay horse now in my possession. In witness whereof I
hereunto subscribe my name and affix my seal this twenty fifth day of April in the year one thousand
eight hundred and thirty eight. 
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of us } Joseph Bishop
State of Alabama } I Joseph Bishop an acting Justice of the peace in and for the
Jackson County } state and county aforesaid do hereby certify that Henry Vance whose
name is subscribed to the foregoing conveyance and who is known to me acknowledged before me on the day
of the date thereof that being informed of the contents of the conveyance he executed the same voluntarily
on the day the same bears date. Given under my hand and seal this 25th day of April 1838.
Joseph Bishop J.P. {Seal}
Recorded the 22nd day of October 1838 and examined by
L. T. Tittle C.C.C. R.M.C.

State of Alabama }
Jackson County } Know all men by these presents that I Michael Brundine
in consideration of the natural love that I have towards my son Hamilton Brundine and
for the further consideration of the sum of one dollar to me in hand paid by the said
Hamilton Brundine have this day given granted bargained sold and conveyed unto the said
and hereby give grant bargain sell and convey unto the said Hamilton Brundine his heirs and
assigns forever the following described tract or parcel of land situate lying and being in the
County of Little River Creek Alabama. Beginning at a point on the south side of the
Little River at a branch known by the name of the fork of the branch 16° 30' E 20 chains
thence N 45° E 20 chains thence N 45° W 20 chains thence S 45° W 20 chains to the place of beginning
containing 40 acres more or less. To have and to hold the said tract or parcel of land with all
and singular the appurtenances thereunto belonging or in any wise appertaining to the only
proper use benefit and behoof of the said Hamilton Brundine his heirs and assigns forever. And I
the said Michael Brundine for myself my heirs executors and administrators do covenant and agree to and
with the said Hamilton Brundine his heirs and assigns that I am lawfully seized in fee of the afore
granted premises that they are free from all incumbrances and that I have a good right to sell and
convey the same to the said Hamilton Brundine as aforesaid and that I will and my heirs executors
and administrators shall warrant and defend the same to the said Hamilton Brundine his heirs and
assigns forever against the lawful claims and demands of all persons. In witness whereof I have hereunto
set my hand and seal this 19th day of April 1838.
Test. John Bruner } Michael Brundine {Seal}
John Bruner } Before me personally appeared John Bruner and
Jackson County } John Bruner who being duly sworn depose and say that they saw Michael
Brundine sign seal and deliver the within conveyance to Hamilton Brundine for the uses and purposes within mentioned and that they
subscribed their names as witnesses thereto in the presence of the said Michael Brundine on the 19th day of April 1838.
Before me L. T. Tittle C.C.
Recorded the 1st day of Novr 1838 and examined by L. T. Tittle C.C.C. R.M.C.
