Whereas a marriage is intended shortly to be had & solemnized

sum of one dollar to them in hand paid by the said Henrick H. Arnold & Jane L. Riggs
at or before the sealing & delivery of these presents the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged
have bargained & sold unto the said John C. Gisham & Alexander Riggs their heirs & assigns
unto John C. Gisham and Alexander Riggs of Pickens District So. Ca. Trust for the uses herein
specified, to secure the same to the said Jane L. Riggs during her natural life, and at her
death to the children of her body or their heirs and also receiving & hearing the right & 
money by title to her intended Husband aforesaid, for her Estate & the necessary care there-
of as aforesaid. And it is further agreed that the said Secretary shall be taken & appraised by him
and Credit, at fair valuation at the rate Henrick H. Arnold be charged with the

shall be held in manner and form part of agreement, and that the Estate, are
to be vested in the said John C. Gisham & Alexander Riggs that they shall be able
to protect the same from Waste. And it is further agreed that the Secretary shall be re-
moved by the said Henrick H. Arnold and John C. Gisham & Alexander Riggs or their
assigns at any time or times when the Trustee or Trustees shall think proper or by
any body or bodies whomsoever. And the said Henrick H. Arnold & Jane L. Riggs
their heirs and assigns, the Estate, and every part thereof unto the said John C. Gisham
& Alexander Riggs their heirs and assigns forever. And the said Henrick H. Arnold & Jane L.
