State of South Carolina I Anne Lesly Justice of the quorum do hereby certify that 
Richard Dubose & Margaret Steele the wife of the within named Testator did 
this day appear before me and after being privately and separately examined 
by me and declaring that she was freely voluntarily and without any compulsion 
dread or fear of any person or persons whomsoever renounce release and forever 
relinquish unto the within named David Morrow his heirs and assigns all her 
interest and estate and also all her right and claim of Dower of in or to and any 
of the premises within mentioned and released. Given under my hand and seal 
this 20th day of March 1840. 
                                                                                      Margaret Steele
                                                                                      (SEAL)
                                                                                      Anne Lesly J.Q.

State of South Carolina Personally appeared William Hewson before me 
Richard Dubose and made oath in due form of law that he saw Nathaniel Hewitt 
sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the within paper for the uses 
and purposes therein mentioned and that he with John H. Gavin in the 
presence of each other subscribed before me this 20th day of March 1840. 
                                                                                      William Hewson
                                                                                      Anne Lesly J.Q.
Recorded the 17th day of March 1840 and examined by me 
                                                                                      M. L. Keith C.C. & M.E.

State of South Carolina Know all men by these presents that I 
Richmond District Nathaniel Hewitt of the State & District 
aforesaid for and in consideration of the sum of five hundred dollars to me in hand 
paid by Lucas M. Maddon assignee of Ephraim Anderson & Company and the said 
Lucas M. Maddon assignee of Ephraim Anderson & Company have bargained sold 
and delivered and by these presents do bargain sell and deliver unto the said Lucas 
M. Maddon assignee of Ephraim Anderson & Company one negro girl slave by the name 
of Patsey Caroline with her increase to have & to hold the said negro girl together 
with her future increase unto the said Lucas M. Maddon his heirs and assigns forever 
and I the said Nathaniel Hewitt do hereby bind myself my heirs executors and 
administrators to warrant and forever defend the said negro girl slave Patsey Caroline 
and her increase unto the said Lucas M. Maddon his heirs and assigns against all 
persons whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof. 
In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 17th day of March 
in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty. 
                                                                                      Nathaniel Hewitt (SEAL)
                                                                                      In presence of 
                                                                                      John H. Gavin
                                                                                      A. L. Keith
