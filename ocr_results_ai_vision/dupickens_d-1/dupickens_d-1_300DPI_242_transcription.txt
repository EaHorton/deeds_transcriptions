Inventory or Schedule annexed of the other Estate of Maria Jane E Biggs to be secured to the
said John Eddelston under a Marriage Contract as therein mentioned. And the said Jane E. Biggs
doth hereby agree to hand over to the said Henrick J. Arnold, the interest and income on
part of the Estate, Real or Personal as will at all times reimburse her Dower, or several
provisions. And it is expressly agreed and further agreed that the said Jane E. Biggs shall reserve
to herself the right of property to and of her own separate Chattels or portion of house hold
stuff, as necessary, over and above whatever is included for the distribution between her
and her children. And it is further agreed that the property of the said Henrick J.
Arnold shall not be liable for the debts of the said Jane E. Biggs which may be contracted
previous to the solemnization of the said, if the said Jane E. Biggs shall depart by
the said Henrick J. Arnold, and the said Henrick J. Arnold shall have existed on the
property of the said Jane E. Biggs, then the said Jane E. Biggs and the said Henrick J. Arnold, Jane
E. Biggs and John Eddelston or either of them, or all or several of them, shall
intend an inventory of the said estate shall be carried into effect as fully and effectually
if the said Jane E. Biggs were fully competent.

In testimony whereof, we the said Henrick J. Arnold,
Maria Jane E. Biggs, and we the said John Eddelston hath set our hands, seals, and
seals as we did, this the 23rd day of December in the year of our Lord one thousand
eight hundred and thirty nine.

Signed, sealed and acknowledged
in presence of us,                             H. J. Arnold
Wm. W. Colcombe,                              Jane E. Biggs
Francis W. Norris,                            (Seal)
John Eddelston
Frederick District S. C. Personally Came William W. Colcombe, Francis W. Norris, and John
Eddelston, and made oath that they saw H. J. Arnold, Jane E. Biggs, and John E.
Eddelston seal and deliver the within instrument as their act and deed, and that they
with Christian read and subscribe the same as acknowledging the above instrument
as expressing their intentions and set forth and that Henrick J. Arnold, with their
acknowledgment, the execution thereof in presence of each other.
