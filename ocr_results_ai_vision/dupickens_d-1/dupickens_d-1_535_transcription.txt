James Henderson of some place the following property viz one Negro woman slave
named Milly about nineteen years of age, and two children one named Nancy about
three years of age, one named Mary about one year of age. To have and to hold all and
singular the said property together with the future increase of the said Milly and
Milly the said James Henderson his heirs and assigns forever. And I the said James
Henderson do warrant and defend the said property to the said James
Smith his Executors administrators and assigns against any or all persons whomsoever.
James Henderson his heirs Executors administrators shall and will at any time hereafter
on demand of the said James Smith his heirs Executors administrators and assigns pay to
one thousand and twenty dollars to satisfy the said James Smith his heirs or assigns
for the valuation of said property as made on the 1st day of March 1841. When the above sale of
sale and every article in the foregoing conveyance shall cease and the Milly and
other increase in full force and virtue. Dated 28th 1842. James Smith
Test. William Robinson

The State of South Carolina
Know all men by these presents that I Josiah Powell of
the District in the State aforesaid for and in consideration
of the sum of seventy Dollars to me in hand paid by the said
William of Richland District and State aforesaid the receipt whereof is hereby
acknowledged, and by these presents do grant bargain and sell unto the said
William Whitmore all that certain parcel or tract of land situate in Richland District
and State aforesaid, said lying on both sides of the north Fork of Little River, originally
granted to Thomas, and beginning at a stake on the Ferry branch and running
thence south to a Chesnut station, thence to a light corner, thence to a pine, thence
thence to a conditional line made by Thomas Fields and Thomas State to Little River
thence up Little River to a stake on the goods, thence to a post oak, thence to a pine
thence to the conditional line line of State, thence to a stake corner on the south, thence
to a stake on the Little Fork of the River, thence to a stake corner, thence to a pine
corner, and said Whitmore and I, Thomas, together with all and singular
the rights members hereditaments and appurtenances thereunto belonging or in any
wise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the said premises
unto the said William Whitmore his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind
myself my heirs executors and administrators to warrant and forever defend all and
singular the said premises unto the said William Whitmore his heirs and assigns
against myself and my heirs and against every person whomsoever lawfully claiming
or to claim the same or any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this 1st day of January
in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two and in the sixty sixth
year of the Independence of the United States of America. Josiah Powell (Seal)
