James Henderson of some place the following property viz one Negro woman Slave
named Milly about nineteen years of age, and two Children one named Fanny about
three years of age, one named Mary about one year of age. To have and to hold all and
singular the said property together with the future increase of the said woman
Milly to the said James Henderson his heirs and assigns forever. Provided
nevertheless if the said James Henderson his heirs and assigns shall pay or cause to be paid
Smith his Executors administrators or assigns the sum of forty five dollars on or before
the first day of January 1842 then these presents to be void and of no effect. But if
default shall be made in the payment of the said sum of forty five dollars or any part
thereof then it shall be lawful for the said Smith his Executors administrators or assigns
to sell and dispose of the said property hereby granted or so much thereof as may be
necessary to satisfy the said sum of forty five dollars with interest and all charges
thereon. In witness whereof the said James Henderson hath hereunto set his hand and
seal this 28th day of August 1841. Signed sealed and delivered in presence of
James L. Smith
M. Smith

Fourteen months after date we or either of us promise to pay James Henderson
Administrator of the Estate of Thos Henderson deceased a home the sum of one thousand
and twenty dollars for value received August 28th 1841.
James L. Smith
M. Smith

South Carolina Before me personally appeared Mary Mills the within named
Richard Outlaw & oath that he saw present and also James Henderson sign seal and
as deliver the within writing for the uses and purposes within mentioned
Sworn to & subscribed this  day of 1842

The State of South Carolina
Know all men by these presents that I Josiah Powell of
the District in the State aforesaid for and in consideration
of the sum of seventy Dollars to me in hand paid by the said
William of Richland District and State aforesaid the receipt whereof is hereby
acknowledged, and by these presents do grant bargain and sell unto the said
William Whitmore all that certain parcel or tract of land situate in Richland District
and State aforesaid, said lying on both sides of the north Fork of Little River, originally
granted to Thomas, and beginning at a stake on the Ferry branch and running
thence south to a Chesnut & station thence to a poplar corner thence to a pine corner
thence to a conditional line made by Thomas Fields and thence to Little River
thence up the River to a stake on the goods thence to a poplar thence to a pine
thence to the conditional line line of Fields thence to a stake corner on the south line
of the original tract to the beginning corner containing one hundred and fifty
acres more or less said Whitmore and I forever. Together with all and singular
the rights members hereditaments and appurtenances thereunto belonging or in any
wise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the said premises
unto the said William Whitmore his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind
myself my heirs executors and administrators to warrant and forever defend all and
singular the said premises unto the said William Whitmore his heirs and assigns
against myself and my heirs and against every person whomsoever lawfully claiming
or to claim the same or any part thereof. Witness my hand and seal this  day of June
in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two and in the sixty sixth
year of the Independence of the United States of America.
Josiah Powell (Seal)
