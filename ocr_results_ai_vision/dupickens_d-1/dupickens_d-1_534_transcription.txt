State of South Carolina, To all whom these presents may
concern William Crenshaw in the State aforesaid
send Greeting. Whereas I the said William Crenshaw in
my certain bond or obligation bearing date the Thirtieth
day of February in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two, stand firmly held and bound
unto A. B. Grant in the penal sum of Two thousand & fifty dollars, conditioned for the payment of the full and just sum of one thousand and twenty five dollars lawful money of the said State aforesaid, with interest from the first day of February last, as in and by the said bond or obligation and condition thereof reference being thereunto had, may more fully appear. Now Know all men by these presents, that I the said William Crenshaw am held and firmly bound unto the said A. B. Grant in the full and just sum of one thousand and twenty five dollars lawful money of the said State aforesaid, to be paid to the said A. B. Grant or his certain attorney, executors, administrators or assigns, to which payment well and truly to be made, I bind myself, my heirs, executors and administrators, and every of them firmly by these presents. Sealed with my seal, and dated the thirtieth day of February in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty two.

The condition of this obligation is such, that if the above bounden William Crenshaw his heirs, executors or administrators, or any of them, shall and do well and truly pay or cause to be paid unto the above named A. B. Grant his certain attorney, executors, administrators or assigns, the full and just sum of one thousand and twenty five dollars lawful money of the said State aforesaid, together with lawful interest for the same, at or upon the thirtieth day of February next ensuing the date hereof, without fraud or further delay, then the above written obligation to be void and of none effect, or else to remain in full force and virtue.

Personally appeared R. Beale Esq. and made oath that he saw
William Crenshaw sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the
within Mortgage for the uses and purposes within mentioned and
J. W. Stokes with himself in the presence of each other.
Sworn to before me this 1st day of July 1842.
W. L. Keith C.C.P.

South Carolina. Know all men by these presents that I James
Smith of the State aforesaid, for and in consideration of the sum of
One thousand and twenty dollars to me in hand paid, at and
before the sealing and delivery of these presents, by James
Henderson of the State aforesaid, the receipt whereof is hereby
acknowledged, have granted, bargained, sold and released, and by
these presents do grant, bargain, sell and release unto the said
James Henderson, all that plantation or tract of Land containing two hundred
and seventy acres more or less, situate, lying and being in Richland District on the Wateree
Creek, bounded by lands of William Crenshaw, John Brown, Elias Davis, and others, having
such shape, form and marks as are represented by a plat hereunto annexed. Together with all and
singular the rights, members, hereditaments and appurtenances to the said premises belonging
or in anywise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the premises before
mentioned unto the said James Henderson his heirs and assigns forever. And I do hereby bind
myself, my heirs, executors and administrators, to warrant and forever defend all and singular
the said premises unto the said James Henderson his heirs and assigns against me and my heirs
and against every person whomsoever lawfully claiming or to claim the same or any part thereof.
Witness my hand and seal this thirtieth day of June in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and forty two, and in the sixty sixth year of the sovereignty and independence
of the United States of America.

Personally appeared R. Beale Esq. and made oath that he saw
James Smith sign seal and as his act and deed deliver the
within Mortgage for the uses and purposes within mentioned and
J. W. Stokes with himself in the presence of each other.
Sworn to before me this 1st day of July 1842.
W. L. Keith C.C.P.
