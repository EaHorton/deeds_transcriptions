State of South Carolina, To all whom these presents may concern William Crenshaw in the State aforesaid send Greeting. Whereas I the said William Crenshaw in my certain bond or obligation bearing date the Thirtieth day of February in the year of our Lord one thousand Eighty two, stand firmly held and bound unto A. B. Grant in the penal sum of Two hundred and fifty dollars, lawful money of the state aforesaid, for the payment of which sum of money, well and truly to be made and done, I bind myself, my heirs, executors and administrators, firmly by these presents. Now Know all men by these presents, that I the said William Crenshaw am held and firmly bound unto the said A. B. Grant in the just and full sum of one hundred and twenty five dollars, lawful money of the state aforesaid, to be paid to the said A. B. Grant, his certain attorney, executors, administrators or assigns, to which payment well and truly to be made, I bind myself, my heirs, executors and administrators, firmly by these presents. Sealed with my seal, and dated the thirtieth day of February in the year of our Lord one thousand Eighty two. The condition of the above obligation is such, that if the above bound William Crenshaw, his heirs, executors or administrators, shall and do well and truly pay, or cause to be paid unto the above named A. B. Grant, his certain attorney, executors, administrators or assigns, the just and full sum of one hundred and twenty five dollars, lawful money of the state aforesaid, on or before the thirtieth day of February next ensuing the date hereof, with lawful interest for the same, then the above obligation to be void, or else to remain in full force and virtue.

South Carolina Personally appeared R. B. Steele Esq. and made oath that he saw William Crenshaw sign, seal and as his act and deed, deliver the within Mortgage for the uses and purposes within mentioned and that he with J. W. Brown subscribe himself a witness to the due execution thereof. Sworn to before me this 1st day of July 1842.
