Thos R. Cherry and made oath that

the purposes therein mentioned
Sworn before me this
27th April 1845
Thos R. Cherry
Edward Carleton N.P. & Q.
Recorded the day of 1845 & Examined by me

presents that I John T.

Dollars, to me in hand paid by Thomas M. Cox of the District of Greenville
in the State aforesaid, the receipt and payment of which I hereby acknow-
ledge, have granted bargained sold and released and by these presents do
grant bargain sell and release unto the said Thomas M. Cox his heirs and
assigns forever. All that piece parcel or tract of land, situate in Pickens

ning thence N. 35 W. 198 to a dead Post oak, thence N. 73 W. 106 to a pine
thence N. 60 W. 80 to a pine, thence N. 40 W. 80 to a black oak, thence N. 48 E. 58 to a pine
