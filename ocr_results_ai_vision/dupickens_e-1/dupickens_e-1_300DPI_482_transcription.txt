In presence of
Eda Hester, Am Holland
State of South Carolina
Pickens District
Before me personally appeared Am Holland and
Eda Hester and being duly sworn upon oath says that he did
see above deed of gift read and properly signed and acknowledged for the use

E. M. Keels Not. Pub. Exoff. M. P. Q.

Vincent Brown
To
Larkin E. Brown
Deed of Gift
State of South Carolina
Pickens District
Know all men by these presents that I
Vincent Brown of the state and District
aforesaid for and in consideration of the natural love and
affection which I bear to Larkin E. Brown and Francis E. Brown of the state
and District aforesaid have given and granted and by these presents do give
and grant unto the said L. E. Brown & F. E. Brown their executors administrators
and assigns forever all my property of every kind and description to hold
the said property unto them the said L. E. Brown and F. E. Brown their executors
administrators and assigns forever. And I the said Vincent Brown
for myself my executors and administrators the said property unto
the said L. E. Brown and F. E. Brown their executors administrators
and assigns against the claim of me the said Vincent Brown my
executors and administrators and of every other person whomsoever
shall and will warrant and forever defend by these presents. In witness I
have hereunto set my hand and seal this 7th day of Sept 1845
Test                                                                                     Vincent P. Brown (Seal)
Martin Francis Thomason
State of South Carolina
Pickens District
