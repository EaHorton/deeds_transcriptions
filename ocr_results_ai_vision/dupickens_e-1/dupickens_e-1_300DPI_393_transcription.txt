Joseph Grisham } of the County of Sampson & State aforesaid

38 Cents due 6 October next the other due 6 October 1847 for one hundred and fifty six

Notes as they become due and the other debts, according to contract, and if I fail the

at the first demand the said Grisham has or may have against him, with the interest
costs and charges the said Grisham his Heirs or Assigns shall have power and is hereby
authorized to take said Female Slave and her issue & sell them at Public
auction for cash or on a credit and to enforce the cost of collection and apply
if any to the said Fountain his heirs or assigns. Witness my hand and seal
3rd Oct 1845. In presence of
Levi R. Robin
