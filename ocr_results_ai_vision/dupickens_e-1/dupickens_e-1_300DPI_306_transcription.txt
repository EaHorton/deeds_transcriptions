Release For Three V85 Acres Land.
The State of South Carolina  Know all men by these presents, that I
Pickens District  James Lay of Pickens District & State
aforesaid for and in consideration of the sum of Eight hundred
Dollars to me in hand paid the receipt thereof is hereby acknowledged by Bennett
Moody of said State & District, have this day bargained sold unto the said Moody
Three tracts of land (to wit) Tract No. 3 lying & being in north Cherokee Creek situate
in Pickens & Anderson Districts on a branch of said Creek the beginning corner of said
tract at stake N 29 W 36.50 to post oak S 69.20.20 to black oak N 16.42.30 to stake N
9.06 E 6.26 to the original corner which by the original survey was numbered 2
the beginning corner also. Another tract of land known by name as the above
tract occupied by J. Aston lying on said Creek B 3.30.30 to B 30.21.10 B 3.30.30 to
B 36.50 to B 36.50 to B 36.50 to B 36.50 to the beginning of the survey
of the first beginning of B 32 to black oak N 4.37.30 W 5.30 to stake N 3.36.30 to
saplin 3k thence N 36 E 15 to stake thence S 83 E 15 to stake thence S 30 E 36.30
to the beginning corner the above described tract, containing 45 per survey of land.
The above described land I do hereby release to said Moody his heirs or assigns
I have and to hold all the rights hereditaments, appurtenances, or in any wise
appertaining unto the above described lots, tracts, & parcels, and I James Lay for
myself my heirs & assigns forever release unto the said Bennett Moody his
heirs administrators assigns the afore described land & parcel by these presents
forever defend & before all & singular the premises before mentioned unto the said
Moody his Heirs & assigns against myself my heirs & assigns against any other person or
persons lawfully claiming the same or any part thereof. In testimony of which I
have hereunto subscribed my name & affixed my seal this the fifth day of December
in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and Forty Two.
Signed sealed & delivered in the presence of
Quincy Hixon
Henry Hixon
James Lay (Seal)

South Carolina Pickens Know me the magistrate, in and for the District aforesaid
Pickens District I do hereby certify that within my commission the Elizabeth
Lay the wife of the within named James Lay did this day appear before me and
upon being privately and separately examined by me did declare that she freely
and voluntarily without any compulsion dread fear of any person or persons whomsoever renounce release forever relinquish unto the within named Bennett
Moody his heirs assigns all her right and claim of Dower of in and to all and
singular the premises within mentioned and released. Given under my hand
and seal this December the 6 day 1812.
Elizabeth Lay
(Seal)
Quincy Hixon J. P. (Seal)
South Carolina Pickens District. Before me personally appeared Quincy Hixon
