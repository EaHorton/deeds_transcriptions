Release For 432½ Acres Land.
State of South Carolina
Know all men by these presents, that I
P. P. Vandever of the District and State aforesaid
for and in consideration of the sum of Four hundred Dollars, to me
paid by James M. Harrison of the same State and District the receipt whereof I hereby
acknowledge, have granted, bargained, sold & released by these presents do grant
bargain sell and release unto the said James M. Harrison a certain tract of land
situate on the District of Pickens State aforesaid, on waters of Big Reedy Fork Creek
adjoining lands of Elias Earle, Martha Tinsley & others, containing Four hundred & thirty
two & half acres, more or less, being that part of the lands of the late James McClees
which fell to his widow Esther McClees on a division between herself & James
Henry McClees, the said Esther late resided, together with all and singular the
rights, members hereditaments, and appurtenances to the said premises belonging
or in any wise incident or appertaining. To have and to hold all and singular the above
mentioned premises unto the said James M. Harrison his heirs & assigns forever
And I do hereby bind myself my heirs executors and administrators to warrant and
forever defend the said premises against myself my heirs and those claiming
from through, or under me. In testimony I have hereunto set my hand and seal
this thirtieth day of September in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred
and Forty five and in the seventieth year of the sovereignty and independence of the
United States of America. In the presence of
J. F. Cunningham John P. Brown  P. P. Vandever (Seal)

State of South Carolina Personally appeared J. F. Cunningham and being duly sworn
saith that he did see P. P. Vandever sign seal and as his act
and deed deliver the within deed to the said James M. Harrison for the uses and purposes
therein mentioned and that he with John P. Brown in the presence of each other witness
to the same sworn and subscribed before me this 2nd Jany 1846.
S. A. Robin C. C. & Not. off. M. C.  J. F. Cunningham
Recorded the 6th day of January 1846 and examined by
W. S. Keels C. C. & M. C.
