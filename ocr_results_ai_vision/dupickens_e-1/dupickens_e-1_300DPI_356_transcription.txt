Release For 38 Acres Land

Pickens District  Bowen of the State and District aforesaid for and
in consideration of the sum of Fifty Dollars to me in hand paid
by Hiram Bowden Junior of the same State and District have bargained
and released as by these presents do grant bargain and convey unto the said
Hiram Bowden his heirs and assigns a certain tract or parcel of land containing
thirty eight & 1/2 acres more or less situated in the State and District aforesaid
on the Waters of Saluda River adjoining land belonging to E. L. Hunt lately, known
as a part of land sold to said Hiram Bowden. Beginning on a Chestnut
Corner, thence N. 10 E. 15.50 to a W. O. thence N. 85 E. 15.50 to a Black
