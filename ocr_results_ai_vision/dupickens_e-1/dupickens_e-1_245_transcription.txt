Before me personally came Jeremiah S. Chastain who being
duly sworn deposeth and sayeth that he saw William Gray
seal and deliver the within deed of Conveyance to Lewis Brown for the use
and purposes therein expressed and that this deponent was a subscribing witness
to the same and saw John M. Stansel do so also as a witness to the same Sworn to
and subscribed before me this 30th October 1844.
                                                                 J. S. Chastain
                                                                 M. H. Gathright J. I. C.

I John M. Pulliam Clerk of the Superior Court of said County do
hereby certify that M. H. Gathright is a Justice of the
Inferior Court of said County and authorized to administer oaths or
such is certified to from Yadkin and I am in hand and seal of Office
this 30th day of October 1844.
                                                                 J. M. Pulliam Clk

Recorded this 8th day of November 1844 and examined by
                                                                 W. L. Keith C. C. C. & N. P.

Know ye by these presents, that I Lewis Brown of
the State aforesaid, for and in consideration
of the natural love and affection which I have and
bear towards my Daughter Edy Leatherwood and in consideration of the
sum of Five Dollars to me in hand paid by J. M. Barton and W. L. Keith
both of Pickens District and State aforesaid, the receipt and payment
whereof is hereby acknowledged, I have bargained and sold and delivered
unto these presents, in bargain and sale and in open market, according
to the said J. M. Barton & W. L. Keith Trustees the following Negro (to wit)
Betty, a Girl about fourteen years of age to have and to hold and enjoy
the said Slave Betty, together with her future increase unto the said J. M.
Barton & W. L. Keith Trustees and to their Heirs and executors
for the only use of and to the benefit and behoof of the said Edy Leatherwood
during her natural life and after her death to the only use benefit and
behoof of the children of the said Edy Leatherwood, and in case of the
plain and open market deliver unto said Trustees, one fifth part of
my real and personal Estate, that is to say property of every kind, title or other
wise, that I may have at the time of my death and that is now in my
use and possession, as they may see fit for the use and benefit of the said Edy
Leatherwood during her life and after her death to the only use and benefit
of her body, and I do hereby bind myself my Heirs and assigns to the
