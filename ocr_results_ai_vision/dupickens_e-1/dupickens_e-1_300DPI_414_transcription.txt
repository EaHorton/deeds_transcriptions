Came and appeared before me Elisha H. Griffin Magistrate for said District
Jinny Barton of said District and after being duly sworn on the Holy Evangel-
ist maketh the following affidavit she says on oath she was called to witness the
gift and delivery of a Negro Girl Caroline to her Daughter Amy Barton now
Amy Power likewise of the late Robert Power from John Fields late of Pickens
District in the month of February one thousand eight hundred and fifteen the
day of the month not recollected she went to her Fathers House with us (Pickens)
and he the said John Fields call[ed] for her daughter Amy he took the Child (Caroline
her) the same called Amy Eliza Caroline he placed her in front of said us in the
road Amy and said bear witness you are a witness this Negro Caroline to
Amy Barton now Amy Power as her own right and property holding their
hands together during her life time, and at her death to be equally divided with
all her increase between the Heirs of her body, Bailey Barton my son James
and Sarah Stephens my daughter Called, now in possession of the State of
Georgia are called on to witness the same transaction. Sworn to and subscribed
this 28th day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred
and forty six before me.
                                                                                      her
                                                                                  Jinny X Barton
                                                                                      mark
E. H. Griffin M. P. (L.S.)
Recorded the 1st day of February 1846 and examined by
                                                                                  W. L. Steele C.C. & M.C.
