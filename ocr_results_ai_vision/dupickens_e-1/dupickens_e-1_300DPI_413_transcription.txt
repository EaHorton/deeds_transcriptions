Pickens District } and made oath in due form of law and on oath saith he saw

State of South Carolina } W. Stewart Magistrate for Pickens District, certify unto all
Pickens District } whom it may concern, that Redman Stephenson the
within named David James, did this day appear before me and upon being privately
and separately examined by me did declare freely voluntarily and without any
compulsion dread or fear of any person or persons whomsoever renounce release
and forever relinquish unto the within named John Young his heirs and assigns
all her interest and estate in all her right and claim of Dower of in or to all and sin-
gular the premises within mentioned and released. Given under my hand and seal this
the day of
W. Stewart J. P. (Seal)

Bailey Barton's Affidavit.
The State of South Carolina }
Pickens District } This day personally came and appeared before me
Elick H. Griffin, Magistrate for said District, Bailey
Barton of said District and after being duly sworn on the Holy Evangelist maketh the
following affidavit he say on his oath he was called on to testify the gift and
delivery of a negro Girl Caledon to his daughter Amy Barton now Amy Bowen
the receiving witness for John Robert Bowen and sworn for said to Pickens District
in the month of February one thousand eight hundred and fifteen the day of the
month not recollected he went to his Father in laws, house in the District and he then
said Jno Fields called for his Grand Daughter Amy he took the Child between his knees
then called for the Girl Caroline he placed the hand of Caledon in the hand of Amy
and said here take this girl Caroline this negro Caroline to Amy Barton now
Amy Bowen as her own right and property holding their hands together giving her
Caledon and her increase to be Equally divided with all her increase forever to the
heirs of her body, Amy Barton, my wife, and Isaiah Kirksey, then after Pickens
District was laid off the State of Georgia in one or two winters after the above
Sworn to and subscribed this 1st day of January one thousand eight hundred and sixteen
night received as a forty for sworn to and Subscribed before me
E. H. Griffin M. P. (Seal)
Bailey Barton
