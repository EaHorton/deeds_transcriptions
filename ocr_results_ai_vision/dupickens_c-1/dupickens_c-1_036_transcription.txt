State of South Carolina	Know all men by these
Anderson District	presents that we the undersigned
legatees of Thomas deceased to wit Elizabeth Jones
& her son Adam & her son William decd give unto Sarah King & his
Grand daughter a certain Negro girl named Peggy and a Negro girl Lucy in
her previous natural life before the death of her Grand father
and we do hereby relinquish all our claim forever in a Negro
Lucy or male that we also relinquish all our claim forever in a Negro
if any we have to the said Sarah King now Sarah Barton and that we do
by these presents assign and convey unto her forever the same
conveyance whereunto we assign our names this 1st May 1831 as Witness
our hands and seals
