Know all men by these presents that we the undersigned  
heirs of the Estate of William deceased late of the  
District and State aforesaid do give unto Sarah King his  
Grand daughter a certain Negro girl named Peggy and a Negro girl Lucy in  
her previous and we do hereby for the interest of him our grand father  
and our own interest being satisfied that such conveyance of said  
Negroes was made that we also relinquish all our claim forever in a Negro  
if any we have to the said Sarah King now Sarah Barton and that we do  
by these presents convey the same to her heirs and assigns to claim to the same  
conveyance whereunto we assign our names this 1st May 1831 as Witness  
our hands and seals  
Test Ransier Anderson Lucinda Liddis

his mark

State of South Carolina Personally appeared before me James L. Anderson and

tioned and that Lucinda Liddis was a subscribing witness with himself  
Sworn to before me this 20 day of May 1831  
George W. Anderson  
Magistrate

William N. Pickens C.C.P. & M.E.
