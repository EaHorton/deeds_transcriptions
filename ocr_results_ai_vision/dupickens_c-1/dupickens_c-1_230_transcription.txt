The State of South Carolina
This Indenture made this sixth day of September in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty six between William Wilson, Esquire, Sheriff of Anderson District in the state aforesaid of the one part and Robert Wilson of the said state and District of the other part. Witnesseth that whereas William Wilson, deceased, in his life time and at the time of his death lawfully seized and possessed of a certain piece or parcel of land containing one hundred and forty five acres more or less situate in Anderson District on the waters of Twelve Mile Creek and whereas the said William Wilson died intestate, as it is said and whereas upon the oath of the said William Wilson the said plantation or tract of land above mentioned was at his decease, after much persuasion and deliberation, divided to his children William, Robert Wilson, Easley Hamilton in right of his wife Jane, heirs of William Wilson, Daniel Wilson, and Cumming, heirs of said William Wilson on the sixth and the fifth day of January in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty five made application to the Court of Common Pleas for the District aforesaid to have his dower set apart in the interest and provisions of an Act of the General Assembly of the state that has been made and provided settlement set apart to give in evidence the judgment of the Court of Ordinary throughout the state in the sale or division of real Estate not exceeding a certain value, directed to Andrew Wilson, Robert Wilson, Easley Hamilton in right of his wife Jane, Heirs of William Wilson, Daniel Wilson, and Cumming, heirs of said William Wilson, and the representatives of the said William Wilson, heirs, deceased requiring them to appear before the Court of Ordinary and the Court of Ordinary to be holden at Pickens Court House for Pickens District on the first Monday in March following the said judgment one thousand eight hundred and thirty five to show cause (if any they could) why the real Estate of William Wilson deceased should not be sold according to the act in such case made and provided.
