South Carolina          January 10th day 1836. To all persons whom it may 
Pickens District          concern that Milly Jackson do agree to bind 
my three children to Patrick Kelley and Susan Kelley Washington 
Jackson and John Jackson and Polleyan Jackson these three 
Children named or written to have and to hold these three Children during their life time 
or her or his, one or both of said Kelley these three Children to be bound to said Kelley till 
they are twenty one years of age. Before they are of age going to see to them if said 
Kelley agree at that time to him and her the two Kelley as named before or above 
written the said Kelley is to give G. Washington Jackson is to have a good horse bridle 
and saddle and two suits of good clothing when he comes of age and said John 
Jackson is to have the same for his Brother a good horse bridle and saddle and two 
suits of good clothing and the said Polleyan Jackson is to have a good feather bed and 
two suits of good clothing and a good bed furniture. 
Test John Colhoon, Wm. Hammit, John Colhoon. 
                                                                                      Milly her Jackson
