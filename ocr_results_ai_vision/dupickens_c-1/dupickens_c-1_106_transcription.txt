I saw this day at the Falls of Little River have the names of two negroes fixed on each
of three tickets of equal size on foolscap & had a person unconnected and
unacquainted with my distressing situation draw from the said Hat for my daughter
Mary Scyfforth the two following workers. Vina and Caroline. For my daughter
Mexican York. Polly & Susan. For my daughter Lucy Stidom. Betsy & Hannah.
Now know all men by these presents that the said Jeffrey Beck for and
in consideration of the natural love and affection which I have and bear towards
my said Daughter Mary Scyfforth & her husband John Scyfforth & have given
and delivered over by these presents a freely given grant and deliver to the said John and
Mary Scyfforth the two slaves Vina and Caroline to have and to hold from
of you are bonafide a female slave and two years and six months of age for this
day of the date of these presents and Mary Scyfforth is to have and to hold
and I do hereby bind myself my heirs executors and administrators to warrant and
defend the said slaves Vina and Caroline from me and my heirs and assigns
to the said John & Mary Scyfforth to hold to them and their heirs forever against
the claims of my heirs assigns and all other persons. Nevertheless I am to retain
all the hire of the said two negroes if I continue to live in the United during
my natural life. Witness my hand and seal this Eleventh day of March
Anno Domini 1835
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of
Joseph Gresham
John Copherhart

I was present and saw write the tickets & saw Hiram Reilly draw them out of the
Hat he knew nothing of what was intended, cannot read kept the Room, or even
of the names written on the tickets. I did not Beck appeared to be very impartial to
decide until it occurred to him that it was the equitable way to determine which of
the negroes should belong to his respective Daughters. I had no opinion or interest
in the matter further than to satisfy and oblige my old friend & Neigh. Witness
Joseph Gresham
State of North Carolina Personally came Joseph Gresham before me J. P.
for said County & made oath that he was present & kept the room
and saw the tickets aforesaid made out in due form of law and no
collusion or fraud in it. Jeffrey Beck also signed and delivered
the said writing and acknowledged the gift for the love and affection he professed
and it is full and true. Frederick N. Gower was with him & gave his previous
acknowledgment to the execution of the same. Sworn to and subscribed
this 11th day of March 1835. Joseph Gresham
J. P. for me F. N. Gower J.P.
Recorded the 1st day of August 1835 and examined by me
William L. Reids C.C. of R.M.C.
