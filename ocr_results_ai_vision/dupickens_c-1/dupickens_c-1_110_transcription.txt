James McKinney
        To
James McKinney

89
The State of South Carolina }
Pickens District              } Whereas James McKinney &
have this day entered into bond
as security for Black James McKinney for six hundred
dollars in order to enter the money cause in the Court of Equity which
James McKinney &ff and Emanuel Colvin deposed and has had to pay the two
hundred one thirty dollars for the said James McKinney making in all eight hundred
one thirty dollars pending in the Court of Equity.
& NOW Know all men by these presents that I Black James McKinney for the better securing
and indemnifying the said James McKinney for the above amount have this day
bargained sold released and conveyed unto the said James McKinney a certain piece or parcel of land situate in
on the said James McKinneys and the said James McKinney and the said James McKinney by his heirs and assigns forever the following property to wit
two Negro girls named Mary and Jane also three head of horses one bay horse Lizzy
two cows, four sheep, two calves, one oxen two bulls
also one bed steed & hangings two Op Tads one lot of Blacksmiths Tools one sorrel
mare one bay horse, one gray horse, one lot of Blacksmiths Tools one lot of
Tools, Stock, Waggon, & harness (one East, Blacksmiths tools one, together with the future
increase of the above should be any to have and to hold the said James McKinney
his heirs and assigns forever and I the said James McKinney for his heirs and
forever defend the above mentioned property to the said James McKinney
his heirs and assigns forever against myself and all persons whomsoever lawfully
claiming the same. Provided always nevertheless and it is expressly agreed by and
between the said parties that if the said James McKinney shall pay and
satisfy the above mentioned Bond and the two hundred one thirty dollars together with
the interest and all the cost that may accrue on the above mentioned suit for which
the said James McKinney has entered in the Court of Equity then this present
and sale to have and no effect and then to the contrary the said James McKinney
shall be at liberty always and at all times to enter into my possession and use said
property to take and convert to his own use settling the said James McKinney
which he is bound to dispose of the same by sale whenever he may see fit
delivering the surplus after all debts costs and charges for which he is bound is
paid to the said James McKinney his heirs or assigns and I do by these presents
the two of June Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty five and in
the fifty ninth year of American Independence
Signed sealed and delivered in presence of
Thomas Bown, Wm P. Deyt
South Carolina } Before me personally appeared Thomas Bown and made
Pickens District } oath that he saw James McKinney sign seal and deliver the within
mortgage to James McKinney for the use and purposes within mentioned and
saw Wm P. Deyt with himself witness the same sworn to this 20th day of August
1835 before William S. Steele C.C. P. & M. E.
Received the 20th day of August 1835 and examined by me
William S. Steele C.C. P. & M. E.
