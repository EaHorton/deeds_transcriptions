Whereas I James McKinney of
have this day entered into bond
as security for Black James McKinney for six hundred
dollars in order to enter the money cause in the Court of Equity which
James McKinney off and Emanuel Colvin deponant and has adjourned to the two
hundred one thirty dollars for the said James McKinney making in all eight hundred
one thirty dollars pending in the Court of Equity
and I now bind all men by these presents that I Black James McKinney for the better securing
and indemnifying the said James McKinney for the above amount have this day
bargained sold released and conveyed unto a person forever all my Estate &
interest the said James McKinney and the said James McKinney doth deliver unto the
said James McKinney his heirs and assigns forever the following property to wit
two Negro girls named Mary and Jane also three head of horses one bay horse Lizzy
two cows, four, steers, five heifers, two Calves and one Bull
also one Bed Stead & hangings two Op Tads one lot of Blacksmiths Tools one Waggon
and one set of Waggon & harness, one lot of Blacksmiths Tools one Waggon, one
Bed, Bedclothes, & hangings, one Cow, East, Blacksmiths tools and, & together with the future
increase of the above should be any to have and to hold the above mentioned
property unto the said James McKinney his heirs and assigns forever and I the said
James McKinney do bind myself firmly by this said James McKinney his heirs
and assigns forever and I am my heirs and assigns forever from any person
observing the same Provided always nevertheless and it is expressly agreed by and
between the said parties that if the said James McKinney shall pay and
satisfy the above mentioned Bond and the two hundred one thirty dollars together with
the interest and all the cost that may accrue on the above mentioned suit in which
the said James McKinney is bound in the Court of Equity then this present writing
and sale to be void and no effect and then to the Contrarily the said James McKinney
shall be at liberty always and at all times to enter into my possession and use said
property to take and convert to his own use settling the said James McKinney
which he pleases to dispose of the same by sale otherwise as he may see fit
delivering the property after all debts costs and charges for which it is bound is
paid in witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal and I do by presents
the 16th day of June one thousand eight hundred and thirty five and in
the fifty ninth year of American Independence
signed sealed and delivered in presence of
Thomas Bown, Wm P. Deyt
South Carolina } Before me personally appeared Thomas Bown and made
Pickens District } oath that he saw James McKinney sign seal and deliver the
mortgage to James McKinney for the use and purposes within mentioned and
saw Wm P. Deyt with himself witness the same sworn to the 20th day of August
1835 before William Steele C.C. P. & M.C.
William Steele C.C. P. & M.C.
Recorded the 20th day of August 1835 and examined by me
William P. Steele C.C. P. & M.C.
